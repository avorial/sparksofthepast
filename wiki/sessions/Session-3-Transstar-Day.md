## Synopsis

The group began in Emma's warehouse with the [[Identity Veils]] nearly spent. They debated whether their first priority should be clearing their names, finding new disguises, pressuring [[Dr. Luc Moreau]], asking [[Inspector Marc Valois]] for help, or sheltering in the [[Transstar Sector Exchange]]. The party leaned away from working directly for [[Marcel Delphine]] and toward getting the recovered evidence into SolSec hands through an intermediary.

One team went back to [[Lucin]], finding him at [[La Porte Fiel]], a drug den or club in Sainte-Liberte. Lucin agreed to cash in the party's favor and arrange delivery of the [[Mobile Data Tube]] evidence, related files, and physical proof to Valois. He offered either a drone drop to Valois's car or a later break-in at Valois's home. The party chose the home delivery, specifically because it would create urgency and make sure Valois personally saw the package. Lucin scrubbed the package for DNA.

During the handoff, [[Mercier's Locked Pad]] suddenly buzzed: "Find My Data Pad" had activated. Lucin sealed it in a Faraday bag, returned it to the party, and the team fled before police reached the club. Ciernan directed the escape route, and the group evaded pursuit.

Meanwhile, Emma, Victor, Dan, and Varian entered the [[Transstar Sector Exchange]] through [[Reena Kessler]]'s gate. They learned that a three-day pass allowed citizens to look for work, but actual employment would make them corporate employees under Transstar protection. That protection would insulate them inside the Exchange from Jardin prosecution while binding them to corporate law.

The first Transstar group decided that even loader work was safer than being caught when the veils failed. They called the rest of the party to bring all gear to the Exchange. Saichi entered as tagged equipment with a restrainer connection that powered down her guns, while Zoy talked his way in without a stamp.

The party took Transstar aptitude tests. Emma excelled at the executive track and became a division-level executive. Several others landed as loaders or security personnel after deciding how far to risk escalating their tests. [[Claire Bouchard]] gathered them for onboarding, issued employee badges with their true faces after VR scans, and gave them thirty days of open employment before a longer contract would be required.

The group was assigned to the [[Aquatic Salvage Project]], a corporate operation involving submersible work and conflict with [[Dolphin Extremists]]. Transstar will house them together in Emma's executive suite on floor 162, and work begins at 4 a.m. The party is safer for now, but clearing their names has become a weekend side job under corporate supervision.
