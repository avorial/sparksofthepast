# Session 5 - The Oduya

**Transcript Source:** `transcript_2026-08-25T13_12_36.595Z.json`

**Caption File:** `wiki/transcripts/Session-5-The-Oduya.srt`

**In-World Date:** 145-1105, evening and night after the Hadal rescue.

## Synopsis

The party regroups aboard the [[TSS Profondeur]] at [[Undersea Base Elyon]] after the tense encounter with the [[SolSec Operative at Elyon]]. [[Zoy (Damon)]] and [[Ciernan Seanislav (Peter)]] debrief the others on the gun-drawn confrontation, while the group agrees that direct pressure on unknown SolSec agents is likely to get someone killed.

[[Emma Colbert (Laura)]] and [[Victor Mendes (Steinbach)]] visit the rescued Hadal survivor in medical care. The survivor wakes, recognizes Victor from the assassination footage, and identifies himself as [[Alain Mercier]]. He explains that high-ranking officials with Platinum 2 security can maintain prepared clone bodies and wake by engram transfer after public death. His restored memory is six weeks old, so he does not know the final evidence his prior self was killed for, but he suspects the Hadal attack was meant to destroy backup bodies before a future assassination.

Mercier cannot legally return to Jardin's surface. If the party keeps him safe until he can leave aboard a Navy vessel, he offers personal access codes to his house and computer. The timing grows worse when the news reports that the Prime Minister's home has been bombed by suspected separatists. The crew realizes that the destroyed Hadal clones may have been preparation for killing someone at ministerial rank or higher.

Victor reframes the old [[Sunken Ship-Boat]] as the answer: if the wreck can pass as a Navy vessel or reach orbit, it may move Mercier off-world without putting him on the surface. Emma convinces Captain [[Etienne Valcourt]] to divert the Profondeur under the cover of continuing rescue and evidence-hunting operations. [[0-31 Saichi Maru (Annette)]] scouts ahead on the sea floor, records the wreck, and finds suspicious removable markings.

The wreck is raised enough to move under its own power. Its real name is [[Oduya]], and its false Navy markings are only a large replaceable wrap. The ship has been underwater for about forty years, with four dead smugglers aboard, including the captain. The party repairs hull breaches, restores partial power, pumps out the water, brings sensors and computers online, and pilots the craft to the surface for air.

Ship logs reveal that Oduya was a smuggling craft selling goods to the [[Jardin Syndicate]] and dolphin contacts. It dove to evade a Navy patrol, was hit by depth charges, and was abandoned after a reactor leak made official recovery unattractive. Saichi finds the dead captain clutching a metallic box containing removable ship transponders. The box gives Oduya multiple identities, including Solomani, Imperium, and Aslan transponder profiles.

The party also recovers cargo: ruined raw materials, destroyed rifles, illegal cybernetic weapons and implants, and a still-living valuable tree in hibernation. Emma identifies [[Anita Singh]] as a contact who can fence the cybernetics for repair services, and [[Sergey Sun]] as a contact who can help move Mercier off-world. By session end, the party has a repairable ship-boat, a way to get Mercier into orbit, and a path toward Mercier's house, computer, and whatever evidence waits beyond Jardin.

## Clues and Revelations

- The Hadal survivor is [[Alain Mercier]], restored through an official clone-and-engram system.
- Platinum 2 clone bodies are grown ahead of time, kept in brain-dead hibernation, and checked for genetic drift.
- Publicly killed officials cannot return to Jardin's surface after restoration; they are meant to leave by Navy ship.
- Mercier's current memory is about six weeks out of date, but he can still provide access to his house and computer.
- Hadal may have been attacked to destroy clone backups before another high-ranking assassination.
- The Prime Minister's home was bombed on the same day Mercier explains this possibility.
- The sunken ship-boat is the smuggling craft [[Oduya]], not a clean Navy vessel.
- Oduya has a modular transponder system with Solomani, Imperium, and Aslan identities.
- Oduya has no jump drive, but it can reach orbit and other planets in the system once repaired.
