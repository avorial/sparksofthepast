# Session 4 - Into the Hadal Deep

**Transcript Source:** `transcript_2026-08-11T13_10_25.568Z.json`

**Caption File:** `wiki/transcripts/Session-4-Into-the-Hadal-Deep.srt`

**In-World Date:** 145-1105, first Transstar workday.

## Synopsis

The party reports for its first shift on the [[Aquatic Salvage Project]] and is assigned to the [[TSS Profondeur]], a refitted former Jardin Navy submersible commanded by [[Étienne Valcourt]]. The new Transstar crew learns the ship layout, meets the senior hands, and starts twelve-on/twelve-off training under [[Maëlle Fournier]], [[Rémi Beauchamp]], and [[Sabine Caron]].

During the run to [[Hadal Research Site]], Emma discovers an encrypted [[Jardinian Naval SOS]] hidden from ordinary Transstar channels. Cass and Varian train on the bridge while Saichi is assigned to drone and hull-response work. The team learns that the opposition is not simply "dolphin extremists": the attackers include humanoids and orcas using cybernetic strength rigs and explosive micro-harpoons.

At Hadal, the site is badly wrecked. Three of five domes are broken, two still hold oxygen, and a drone survey finds a live power station feeding an interior load. The group identifies a sealed habitat chamber behind a damaged airlock and chooses a manned salvage rescue instead of simply breaching and flooding it.

Dan and Victor help rig a temporary airlock seal while Ciernan, Cass, and others enter. Inside are four starving survivors and ten dead Jardin Navy personnel. The crew uses rescue tubes from the Profondeur and hauls the survivors back, but only one lives: a naval officer who looks almost exactly like assassinated Genetics Minister [[Alain Mercier]].

Valcourt diverts to [[Undersea Base Elyon]], where the survivor receives emergency nanite treatment. The discovery raises a new question: why was a full Jardin Navy team trapped inside a Transstar research facility, and why does the surviving officer look like Mercier? Saichi watches the survivor's room and spots a [[SolSec]] operative taking a hand and bone-growth scan. Victor and Dan sabotage base communications to delay outgoing reports, while Zoy and Ciernan try to intercept the SolSec agent and learn exactly how dangerous this thread has become.

1. Transstar badges summon the party to Dock 6-2 for its first [[Aquatic Salvage Project]] shift.
2. The group receives Transstar uniforms, including a visible corporate treatment for Saichi.
3. The party boards the [[TSS Profondeur]], a former Jardin Navy submersible now used by Transstar.
4. [[Étienne Valcourt]] introduces senior crew: [[Maëlle Fournier]], [[Rémi Beauchamp]], and [[Sabine Caron]].
5. The party learns pressure-suit procedures, underwater combat limits, and the dangers of ordinary weapons inside a soft-hulled submersible.
6. Emma uncovers a hidden encrypted [[Jardinian Naval SOS]] tied to the attacked Hadal site.
7. The Profondeur reaches [[Hadal Research Site]] and surveys wrecked domes, damaged airlocks, and remaining geothermal power.
8. The team identifies a sealed oxygenated room and chooses a temporary-airlock rescue plan.
9. Ciernan, Cass, and others enter the sealed room; Dan and Victor help keep the seal and rescue process viable.
10. Four survivors and ten dead Jardin Navy personnel are found inside a Transstar facility.
11. The crew recovers generators, computer gear, data cubes, and a Seafarer skill chip during salvage.
12. Three survivors die after rescue; the last survivor resembles [[Alain Mercier]] and is stabilized.
13. Valcourt diverts to [[Undersea Base Elyon]] for emergency care rather than returning to the coast.
14. Valcourt warns the party that Jardin Navy personnel inside a Transstar site suggest a covert military operation.
15. Saichi observes a [[SolSec]] operative scanning the survivor's hand and reports the contact.
16. Victor and Dan flood communications batteries with salt water to slow outgoing traffic.
17. Zoy and Ciernan intercept the SolSec agent; the agent draws on Zoy and warns the party away from the investigation.

## Clues and Revelations

- The Profondeur is under-crewed because experienced crew were split off to train another vessel.
- The Profondeur's missile room has been converted into pressure rescue pods.
- Prior salvage records include equipment, oxygenators, power couplers, and scrubbed rescue logs.
- Hidden energy weapons aboard the Profondeur are logged as welders.
- Hadal was attacked weeks ago, but a group of Jardin Navy personnel survived trapped inside one oxygenated dome.
- The survivor looks almost identical to [[Alain Mercier]] and wears a Jardin Navy uniform.
- SolSec knows enough about genetic duplication, altered bodies, or clones to perform bone-growth and hand-structure scans.
- Undersea Base Elyon is Transstar-owned, but SolSec can still operate there.
