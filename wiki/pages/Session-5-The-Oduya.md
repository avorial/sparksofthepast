# Session 5 - The Oduya

The full session record is stored in `wiki/sessions/Session-5-The-Oduya.md`, with captions at `wiki/transcripts/Session-5-The-Oduya.srt`.

## Synopsis

The rescued Hadal survivor wakes at [[Undersea Base Elyon]] and identifies himself as [[Alain Mercier]], restored through the [[Clone Rebirth Program]]. He offers access codes to his house and computer if the party can keep him safe and get him legally off-world.

The crew uses the [[TSS Profondeur]] to reach the [[Sunken Ship-Boat]], raises the wreck, and learns its true name: [[Oduya]]. The ship is a forty-year-old smuggler craft with modular false identities, dead crew, illegal cybernetic cargo, and enough potential to become the party's route into orbit and the wider system.
