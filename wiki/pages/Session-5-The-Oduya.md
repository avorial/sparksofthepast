# Session 5 - The Oduya

**Transcript Source:** `transcript_2026-08-25T13_12_36.595Z.json`

**Caption File:** `wiki/transcripts/Session-5-The-Oduya.srt`

**In-World Date:** 145-1105, evening and night after the Hadal rescue.

## Synopsis

The party regroups at [[Undersea Base Elyon]] after the failed attempt to question the [[SolSec Operative at Elyon]]. [[Zoy (Damon)]] and [[Ciernan Seanislav (Peter)]] explain that the operative drew a gun and made clear that SolSec will not tolerate the party interfering. The group agrees that trying to pressure unknown SolSec agents is a good way to get killed.

[[Emma Colbert (Laura)]] and [[Victor Mendes (Steinbach)]] visit the Hadal survivor in the medical bay. Emma clears nearby staff with a convincing false emergency, while Victor wakes the patient carefully. The survivor panics at first because he has seen the assassination footage and believes Victor was one of the people who killed him. He identifies himself as [[Alain Mercier]].

Mercier explains the [[Clone Rebirth Program]]: people with Platinum 2 security and comparable status can maintain prepared clone bodies. When the original dies, memory and identity patterns are transferred into the prepared body. His restored memory is about six weeks old, so he does not remember the final evidence his prior body was killed to suppress. He says the attack on [[Hadal Research Site]] may have been a preparatory strike, destroying clone backups before a future assassination.

The warning lands hard when news breaks that the Prime Minister's home was bombed by suspected separatists. Mercier says anyone publicly killed and restored cannot return to Jardin's surface; legally, he must leave by Navy ship. He offers the party access codes to his house and computer if they keep him safe and get him to an off-world or Navy transfer.

Victor connects that legal need to the old [[Sunken Ship-Boat]] lead. If the party can raise the wreck, it may be able to carry Mercier to orbit without putting him back on the surface. Emma convinces Captain [[Etienne Valcourt]] to divert the [[TSS Profondeur]] under the cover of continued Hadal rescue and evidence-hunting operations.

[[0-31 Saichi Maru (Annette)]] scouts ahead on the sea floor, reaching the wreck before the Profondeur. She records the hull, dead crew, pressure damage, and a suspicious removable Navy marking. When the party arrives, they suit up, patch the hull, restore partial power, drain the water, boot the computer, bring sensors online, and get the craft moving. The wreck's true name is [[Oduya]].

Oduya proves to be a forty-year-old smuggling ship-boat, not a clean Navy vessel. Its logs show it was doing drops for the [[Jardin Syndicate]] and dolphin contacts when a Navy patrol caught it. The ship dove to escape the surface craft, but the patrol used depth charges. The pressure shock injured the crew beyond recovery, and an old reactor leak made official salvage unattractive.

Saichi finds the dead captain in a locker holding a metallic case: the [[Transponder Swap Box]]. The modules inside can swap Oduya's ship identity, including Solomani, old Solomani, Imperium, and Aslan transponder profiles. The party also recovers illegal cybernetic weapons and implants, a valuable living tree in hibernation, ruined raw materials, and destroyed rifles.

Emma identifies [[Anita Singh]] as the best contact to turn the cybernetics into repair services for Oduya, and [[Sergey Sun]] as the contact most useful for moving Mercier. By session end, the party has a named ship-boat, a way to get Mercier into orbit, and a route toward Mercier's house, computer, and the next layer of the conspiracy.

### Clone Law

- Platinum 2 restoration is expensive and reserved for elites.
- Clone bodies are grown ahead of time, kept in brain-dead hibernation, and checked for genetic drift.
- Restored bodies retain the same biological age as the original; younger-body transfer is illegal.
- SolSec can test clone status through growth history, bone and hand structure, genetic identity, and mitochondrial age patterns.
- Publicly dead restored people cannot return to Jardin's surface and are supposed to leave by Navy ship.

### Hadal Implications

- Mercier's memory is about six weeks old.
- He suspects his prior self found evidence after the last memory update.
- Hadal contained political, naval, and senior corporate clone backups in separated areas.
- The Hadal attack may have been aimed at deleting backups before killing originals.
- The [[Prime Minister Bombing]] happens the same day the party learns this theory.

### Valcourt and the Salvage Run

- [[Etienne Valcourt]] is refueling, replacing air filters, removing unneeded research gear, handling paperwork, and recovering bounties for the rescue.
- Emma privately pitches the diversion as a Transstar-interest problem and a legal route for Mercier.
- Valcourt agrees to treat the trip as continued rescue operations and a search for Hadal attackers.

### Saichi's Recon

- She documents the exterior before the Profondeur arrives.
- The ship's apparent Navy marking is a removable wrap, not permanent paint.
- Sea growth suggests the wreck has been underwater for about forty years.
- Inside are dead crew in intact vac suits, killed by violent trauma rather than suit breach.

### Raising Oduya

- The party boards in vac suits with repair kits, engineering tools, electronics tools, and harpoon guns.
- Hull breaches are patched well enough for pressure.
- Power is restored for only a few hours.
- Water is pumped out, but oxygen is not restored until the craft surfaces.
- Computers and sensors come online despite damaged displays.
- The Profondeur leaves extra oxygen and pulls away as a decoy when an unresponsive small craft approaches.
- The ship begins moving under its own power and is piloted to the surface for air.

### Logs and Cargo

- A Navy patrol caught it during a drop and hit it with depth charges.
- The captain died in a locker while holding the [[Transponder Swap Box]].
- Cargo includes ruined raw materials, water-destroyed autorifles, illegal cybernetic weapons and implants, and a living tree in hibernation.
- The cybernetics are valuable enough to fund repairs if sold through the right contact.
