# Providence

## Overview

Providence is a planet in the Jardin system, described in [[Session 6 - Ghosts in the Bloodline]] as one world closer to the sun than Jardin. [[Alain Mercier]] bought an off-world ticket there shortly before his assassination, but the ticket was used by someone else.

## Session 6 Role

The party learns from [[Mercier's Locked Pad]] that the Providence ticket was one of Mercier's most recent items. [[Emma Colbert (Laura)]] uses [[Mark Duncan]] to retrieve the transport record and identifies the passenger as [[Camille Dufour]], an encrypted-identity SolSec agent.

Mercier appears to have sent Camille to Providence as part of a pincer move: while he presented findings to the Senate, she would ask a head or executive of [[Social Solidarity]] to answer for the same accusations.

## Known Details

- Providence is in the same system as Jardin.
- Messages between Providence and Jardin can travel in about five to ten minutes.
- A Social Solidarity executive or head is present there.
- Camille Dufour went there using a ticket purchased by Mercier.
- The Providence lead is the party's clearest next off-world investigative route.
