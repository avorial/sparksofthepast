# Relay Station 932

## Overview

Relay Station 932 is the undersea communications relay described during the [[TSS Profondeur]] briefing. It serves as a major communications hub, passing traffic between undersea ships, bases, and orbital systems.

## Session 4

The relay helps define the limits of communication during undersea operations. Delays and routing constraints become important once the party wants to slow or observe messages about the [[Mercier Lookalike Survivor]].
