# Claire Montfort

![Claire Montfort portrait](/wiki/media/Claire-Montfort.jpg)

*Administrative Director*

[[Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain]] identifies Montfort as administrative director at the [[Free Arden Research Facility]]. She controls access, staff assignments, research support, and allocation of supplies.

[[Professor Lucien Moreau]] contacts her angrily after accepting Cass's cover as a new scientist who has not received equipment or credentials. The party later reads staff material about her role. She is not directly met or confirmed to uncover their deception. The original roster confirms Claire as her first name; Clary is retained as a transcript alias.

:::gm
## Original GM Profile

**Role:** Administrative Director | **Age:** 42 | **Species:** Human.

Polite, ambitious, bureaucratic, and cautious. She recognizes financial irregularities and false personnel records but avoids learning their purpose. She can expose staff rosters, off-book supply contracts, and funding organizations. The original plan proposed her as an early inspection obstacle; in Game 9 Moreau calls her while the party remains under cover.

**Suggested Traveller skills:** Admin 4, Advocate 2, Broker 2, Deception 2, Computers 1.
