# Clary Montfort

[[Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain]] identifies Montfort as administrative director at the [[Free Arden Research Facility]]. She controls access, staff assignments, research support, and allocation of supplies.

[[Professor Moreau]] contacts her angrily after accepting Cass's cover as a new scientist who has not received equipment or credentials. The party later reads staff material about her role. She is not directly met or confirmed to uncover their deception. Her first name is preserved from the transcript's Clary rendering.
