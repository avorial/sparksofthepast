# HMS Hank Aaron

In [[Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain]], this small Jardinian carrier responds to a real distress call forwarded by the crew under the false SolSec identity S42. It acknowledges the request in about forty-five seconds and approaches at an 8G burn.

The ship is described as having three fighter deployments; two fighters are explicitly seen attacking a pirate vessel near [[Free Arden]]. When the pirate dives into the gas giant, Hank Aaron fires missiles that destroy it. The merchant [[Snowball]] survives.

The carrier thanks the apparent SolSec crew, departs at a gentler burn, and is seen again while the Travellers leave for Jardin. Its commander is not named, and a complete armament or tonnage is not established. It does not knowingly provide a Marine extraction for Bellinger; her belief that it might have done so is mistaken.
