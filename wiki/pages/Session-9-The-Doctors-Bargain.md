# Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain

**Source:** `transcript_2026-11-03T14_33_55.474Z.txt`. The recording explicitly identifies Session 9. Its opening recounts [[Session 8 - The Price of Clearing Your Name]]; those events are not repeated operations.

**Subtitles:** [Game 9 SRT](../transcripts/Session-9-The-Doctors-Bargain.srt).

**Chronology:** The crew continues its roughly twenty-hour flight from North Meridian toward Free Arden's biological-research moon. No exact in-world calendar date is given. The moon is rendered Dorina/Dorria/Dorriria in this transcript; [[Domirina]] remains the existing wiki's working entry, with the naming uncertainty retained.

## Synopsis

Still carrying the sensitive records obtained from the [[North Meridian Intelligence Vault]], the Travellers continue toward [[Free Arden]]. They decide that pursuing the investigation is more urgent than returning for another Transstar workweek. [[Emma Colbert (Laura)]] sends a tactful resignation while the crew is still within its initial thirty-day open-employment period. The group hopes to avoid creating a new enemy and leaves future subcontracting as a wish rather than an agreed arrangement. No hostile corporate response or confirmed release of every obligation is received.

On approach, [[Victor Mendes (Steinbach)]] detects a transmitting probe and Emma decodes its distress call. The merchant ship [[Snowball]] has been hiding from pirates inside the gas giant for twenty-two hours and needs naval assistance. The Travellers want to help without inviting scrutiny of their fugitive identities. Victor proposes using the [[Transponder Swap Box]] to pose as a forward SolSec vessel while also relaying the genuine distress report. With [[Cass Pien (Kolb)]] helping the sensor/comms work, the crew broadcasts under the identity S42. The [[HMS Hank Aaron]], a small carrier, responds and accelerates toward the area.

The carrier's arrival flushes out the freighter and its pursuer. Fighters attack the pirate vessel; when it dives into the gas giant, Hank Aaron finishes it with a missile volley. Snowball thanks the Travellers and limps toward Jardin, visibly damaged and leaking hydrogen but expected to survive. The crew continues its mission, retaining the SolSec transponder long enough to avoid an obvious identity change under naval observation.

The research moon has solar panels covering roughly a quarter of its surface. [[Zoy (Damon)]] pilots Volante into a crater without being detected, leaving the ship at about half fuel. [[0-31 Saichi Maru (Annette)]] scouts ahead much faster than the suited biological crew. She identifies a supplies landing shaft and a personnel/service elevator, then gains entry by answering an automated lost-pass prompt that identifies her as non-biological. Two technicians downstairs assume she is a patrol droid needing a replacement credential. Saichi persuades them to accompany her back to the surface to help her cohort.

The deception collapses when a technician sees Zoy and reaches for a weapon. Saichi kills him with her laser pistol; Victor shoots the second technician after Saichi's follow-up misses. The crew uses the dead technicians' biometric access to enter the facility. Zoy finds RFID keys, and Victor recovers an active communicator. The team sends a reassuring text from the dead technician Alex's own pad, buying a fifteen-minute interval before another check-in. Inside the oxygenated facility, Dan modifies a small loader so Zoy can hide inside and pass as a service robot.

Cass scouts ahead, fails to remain unseen, and meets [[Professor Moreau]]. Posing as newly arrived Dr. Penn, he persuades Moreau that administrative failures explain the group's missing uniforms and credentials. Moreau complains to [[Clary Montfort]] and directs them to the medical staff quarters. The Travellers take scrubs and lab coats. In a still-warm incinerator they find titanium spectacle frames matching Moreau's glasses. Later records explain the unsettling clue: researchers rapidly clone themselves to meet deadlines, work alongside their short-lived copies, and burn the bodies when the copies fail.

The crew enters [[Dr. Annalis Bellinger]]'s room using the stolen keys and finds an active terminal. Emma studies the staff records and emails, learning about security commandant [[Remy Luc]], research chief [[Dr. Vassar]], administrator Montfort, caretaker [[Mariel Fonter]], engineer [[Nicholas Perrin]], and confirmed psychic trainer [[Sabine Delaroy]]. Records also identify clones held behind anti-psychic security. With Cass's leadership, Emma inserts staff identities for herself, Cass, and Victor; an attempt to add Dan does not succeed. Zoy's further computer attempt locks the terminal, prompting the group to move.

Dan locates a sequencing room, where Bellinger welcomes them as the new team she has been awaiting. She demonstrates a method for modeling a person's genetic history and likely development, then explains the shortage of [[GL15]], the Krypton-derived substance that permits stable cloning and relatively clean genetic alteration. Substitute methods damage genetic pathways and create new failures even when the researchers can predict the original problem. Much of the useful knowledge is secured in Bellinger's eidetic memory instead of openly accessible files.

Bellinger notices Dan's unsuitable shoes and realizes the visitors are not the doctors they claim to be. Emma reads her reaction correctly: the scientist wants a route out and fears being left as a disposable liability. Claiming a discreet presidential investigation, the crew offers provisional amnesty and extraction. Bellinger admits that her memorized genetic models were read by psychic agents and used to alter political families. She says she has a recording of Social Solidarity delivering target instructions, and agrees to take the research and unlock it after reaching safety. The party has no actual presidential authority to guarantee her requested pardon.

To create an escape opportunity, Bellinger opens the clone containment doors. The facility erupts into gunfire and explosions as dangerous experimental subjects are released. The crew retrieves its original clothes to avoid leaving genetic evidence, and Bellinger arms herself with a gauss pistol. At the service exit, an armed defender is fighting a superhumanly fast sword wielder. Saichi incapacitates the shooter with her heavy rifle. The party fires at the swordsman, but bullets pass through him; he drops through the floor and leaves the elevator path clear.

The crew puts its suits back on. Bellinger has only an emergency suit with forty-five minutes to an hour of air, too little for the biological team's full walk back. Saichi carries her rapidly to Volante while the others follow. Everyone reaches the ship. With enough fuel to return to Jardin but not to extend the trip toward Terra Nova, the crew departs for home with Bellinger and her encrypted research. They intend to present the evidence to the president, but the session ends in flight: there is no presidential audience, pardon, or completed exposure of the conspiracy yet.

### Bellinger's Demonstration

- Dan uses Medic to identify a sequencing area. Entry includes a decontamination spray.
- Bellinger greets the group as new staff and says it has been nine months since fresh faces arrived.
- She describes seven years of her own work building on earlier models of genetic development.
- Her quantum/temporal modeling extrapolates development and reconstructs likely genetic history from samples. It does not establish that she literally transports people through time.
- Ideally samples are available at intervals of about seven years; even a single sample can help reconstruct likely changes over that period.
- The goal includes predicting wear, cancer risk, and other failures, then making preventative changes.
- GL15, derived from the material on Krypton, enables stable modification and cloning. Substitutes force pathways open and damage RNA, producing new fragility and deterioration.
- Predicting a future problem is not equivalent to safely correcting it without GL15. Dan's final suggestion is answered with this distinction.
- Most research access remains locked because Bellinger holds a complex mathematical key and critical knowledge in her eidetic memory.

## Podcast Description

Leaving their Transstar jobs behind, the crew answers a distress call with a daring bluff before slipping into a secret research facility beneath Free Arden's moon. Stolen identities and a robot disguise carry them past the first barriers, but the laboratories conceal disposable copies, engineered psychics, and a scientist who knows how the conspiracy alters its victims. She offers the evidence they need in exchange for escape and amnesty, forcing the Travellers to bargain with someone deeply involved in the crimes they hope to expose. When containment opens and the facility erupts into violence, getting their witness home becomes a race against bullets, dwindling air, and something that can walk through walls.
