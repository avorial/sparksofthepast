# Dr. Annalis Bellinger

## Identity and Expertise

Bellinger is a molecular biologist at the [[Free Arden Research Facility]], encountered in [[Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain]]. The transcript initially renders her logged-in name Annalis Bollinger and later uses Bellinger/Ballinger; this entry uses Bellinger consistently without asserting a corrected first-name spelling.

Recruited for military research at sixteen, she has exceptional memory and skill with chemical bonds. Her eidetic memory stores sensitive research patterns and the mathematical key to her protected data. She has personally worked on genetic-history modeling for seven years, building on prior research.

## Scientific Work

She reconstructs likely genetic changes and predicts later deterioration, aiming to prevent injuries, cancer, and other problems. GL15 allows clean implementation of alterations; substitute processes injure pathways and cause new failures. The models predict problems but do not themselves solve the delivery difficulty.

She admits to memorizing political targets' exact genetic patterns so psychic agents could read them from her mind and alter those people. She says she holds a recording of [[Social Solidarity]] delivering target instructions. These are admissions and evidence claims to be tested, not a blanket declaration of innocence.

## Encounter and Bargain

The crew first uses her unlocked room terminal, then meets her in a sequencing lab. She welcomes their supposed new team before Dan's boots reveal the disguise. Emma realizes Bellinger wants out and fears becoming a scapegoat. Bellinger requests written amnesty; the party claims presidential backing and promises provisional protection, but has no actual presidential pardon to issue.

She loads selected research onto a wrist device, retains the unlock code mentally, opens clone containment to create a diversion, and retrieves a gauss pistol. Saichi carries her to Volante within the emergency suit's short air limit. At session end she is aboard and heading to Jardin. Her research remains locked and the president has not ruled on her bargain.
