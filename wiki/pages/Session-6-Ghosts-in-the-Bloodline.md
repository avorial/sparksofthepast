# Session 6 - Ghosts in the Bloodline

**Transcript Source:** `transcript_2026-09-08T13_39_44.453Z.json`

**Caption File:** `wiki/transcripts/Session-6-Ghosts-in-the-Bloodline.srt`

**In-World Date:** About one week into the party's thirty-day [[Transstar]] work window; likely 152-1105.

## Synopsis

The session opens with the party aboard the freshly raised [[Oduya]], now also entered in Foundry as the [[Oduya|Volante]]. The craft is technically breathable and potentially worth millions, but it is foul, waterlogged, old, and full of undocumented modifications. Diagnostics confirm that the party has acquired a valuable ship-boat with bad thrusters, unreliable power, a damaged sensor suite, old smuggler reputation, a hacked ghost transponder, and a long maintenance history that somehow kept the important machinery alive.

The group decides not to roleplay every salvage sale and component repair. Instead, they sell damaged cargo and salvage rights into repairs, getting Oduya patched, fueled, legally certified, and barely orbit-worthy in about two days. [[Emma Colbert (Laura)]] uses paperwork and contact cover to avoid putting the vessel directly under the party's exposed identities, while [[Varian Svazz (Allen)]] is capable of flying it sub-orbitally to the repair site. The craft remains deeply unpleasant to live aboard: damp, stained, corroded, smelling of seawater, mildew, hydraulic fluid, ozone, and old machinery.

Back in the Transstar apartment, the party finally opens [[Mercier's Locked Pad]] with the code provided by restored [[Alain Mercier]]. The pad greets the user as Senator Mercier and links into a government AI with siloed accounts for political figures. A good computer read confirms that anyone using the correct code can access it for now, but once the system registers Mercier's clone as having left planet, the senatorial account will seal permanently.

The latest edited items are a used off-world ticket to [[Providence]], a draft of Mercier's committee speech, and his research topics. The ticket was not used by Mercier. It was purchased for someone else, later identified through [[Mark Duncan]] as [[Camille Dufour]], an encrypted-identity SolSec agent in a black uniform. The implication is that Mercier sent a trusted SolSec person ahead to Providence to confront or interrogate a head or executive of [[Social Solidarity]] while Mercier presented his findings at the Senate.

The research reveals the heart of the conspiracy. Mercier discovered that certain elite bloodlines were not merely born with inconvenient genetic markers; they were being actively changed over time. The changes are too subtle for routine scans or quick blood tests and only appear in full sequencing for major status gates such as elite university placement, elected office, or military command. Mercier believed this would become blackmail against a society obsessed with genetic purity: a chancellor's supposedly perfect son had already tested unacceptable, and Mercier preserved paired samples from the President of Jardin's son to show changes across time.

The psionic element completes the picture. Mercier's research connects the changes to a hidden [[Psychic Clone Conspiracy]] inside or near Social Solidarity. The faction appears to have learned that hard-cloning known psykers into children can produce psionic results in roughly one out of six bodies. Those psykers developed a new ability that can mentally alter another person's biology over time, pushing changes through marrow, blood, and cell replacement until a large part of the victim's body carries the alteration. This gives the party a plausible explanation for the impossible weapon behavior during the [[Mercier Assassination]] and why the frame-up may have involved more than normal technology.

The party debates what to do with the data. [[Cass Pien (Kolb)]] reveals two useful allies: an influential Solomani Party member and the crusading journalist [[Roman Juma]]. The group rejects sending a journalist to burglarize Mercier's residence, both because it could get the contact killed and because the proof has not yet been recovered. Instead, they settle on a dead-man switch: if Cass does not check in every 24 or 48 hours, or if he sends a duress code, the data goes out through the journalist and political contact. Because one channel is governmental/political and the other journalistic, the GM confirms the release would be hard for Jardin to block once it leaves through a lower-authority world and begins spreading through the sector.

Four members go to [[Mercier Penthouse]]: [[0-31 Saichi Maru (Annette)]], [[Cass Pien (Kolb)]], [[Dan Araujo (Josh)]], and [[Victor Mendes (Steinbach)]]. Emma, Zoy, Varian, Judith, and others remain back or continue study/preparation. The away team uses Mercier's personal code and rides an elevator that opens directly into the penthouse. As the doors open, they hear movement stop inside.

Saichi uses her gecko grippers to fight from above, climbing up and out of the elevator so incoming fire will target her armor rather than the softer party members. Cass follows low in a combat stance. The apparent [[Home Guard]] intruders open fire. Victor takes a 21-damage shotgun blast to the chest and drops unconscious after losing all Endurance in the hit. Saichi absorbs a shotgun hit through her heavy robot frame but suffers serious damage. Dan immediately uses first aid to stabilize Victor and bring him back from bleeding out. Cass and Victor return fire, Saichi kills or disables the last attacker with her laser rifle, and the team secures the Home Guard bodies, comms, and cuffs before backup can arrive.

The search pays off. A strong Recon result finds a secret door behind the television. Inside is the [[Mercier Evidence Cache]]: five physical genetic comparison samples, research explaining how the changes happen, photos of suspected Social Solidarity opposition figures, a hidden communicator, a laser pistol similar to Cass's, and about Cr200,000. The samples are blood drops on slides with readouts, making them evidence rather than mere copied data.

The injured team escapes back to the Transstar apartment in shredded uniforms and with visible wounds. First aid can no longer solve Victor's deeper physical-stat damage, so the group performs surgery on the dining room table rather than risk a formal medical facility. Leadership and Zoy's aid cancel the improvised-surgery penalty, and Allen's strong medic roll removes the worst pellets and restores Victor to functional condition. Dan performs emergency mechanical/robotic work on Saichi, stopping leaks but not doing a perfect repair. The party later gets Saichi professionally repaired through Transstar channels, leaving her with visible Transstar-branded repair plates.

By the end of the session, the party has the proof Mercier wanted, a dead-man switch, Cr200,000, an ugly but useful ship-boat, and a clear next problem. [[Alain Mercier]] is still vulnerable at [[Undersea Base Elyon]], and the scan by a SolSec agent means the secret may already be moving through government channels. Emma uses administrative authority to hide Mercier on the [[TSS Profondeur]] as cargo or a maintenance assignment. The plan for next game is to continue under Transstar cover, return to Profondeur operations, guard Mercier, pay docking fees for Oduya, and prepare for the Providence lead.

## Scene Notes

### Ship Quirks and Oduya's New Status

- Oduya is entered in Foundry as **Volante**, with Laura on sensors, Victor/Steinbach on engineer, and Varian on pilot.
- The bridge has only one true seat: the pilot's acceleration/gravity seat. Everyone else stands or works at stations.
- Damaged thrusters impose -1 on all out-of-water pilot checks.
- Above 3G, even in space, a noticeable vibration runs through the ship.
- Temperamental systems mean electronics or mechanics checks to diagnose problems lose one success, though repairs are not harder after diagnosis.
- Repairs are difficult because many components come from later models, civilian systems, or parts never meant to fit.
- Asymmetric drive response can force an extra -1 pilot check during emergency acceleration or violent evasive maneuvers; failure makes the ship tilt.
- Older spacers, smugglers, fencers, and customs officers may recognize the hull configuration and old reputation.
- Underworld contacts get +1 on social checks involving the ship, but law enforcement recognition may require explanations.
- Damaged sensors impose -1 on electronic sensor checks; short-range and internal scans work, but passive sensors have an aft-port blind spot.
- The ghost transponder can be coded on the fly with Electronics (Computers), giving +1 success to disguise identity, but it may pull scanned registrations with unknown trouble attached.
- Oduya is well maintained despite everything: all maintenance costs are halved because someone obsessively cared for the vital machinery.

### Repair Deal

- After cargo/salvage sale, the hull is patched and pressure-tight with mismatched plating and sealant visible around breaches.
- Fuel tanks are flushed and filled, giving enough fuel for normal operations.
- Startup power remains unreliable and may take multiple attempts before the plant stabilizes.
- Life support is technically operational but unpleasant; filters and ducting are cleaned rather than replaced.
- Interiors remain damp, stained, and water-damaged.
- Cargo areas are usable but still corroded and wet in places.
- Crew spaces are barely habitable; mattresses and soft furnishings should be replaced.
- The repair yard can make Oduya orbit-worthy in two days.
- Oduya is still roughly a Cr2.5 million asset despite its terrible condition.
- It has no jump drive, but it has bare-bones versions of the other systems a small craft needs.
- It has a turret mount but no installed gun.

### Genetic Alteration Discovery

- The investigation began about two and a half years ago.
- A chancellor's son registered for an elite Solomani university and came back genetically unacceptable despite two supposedly ideal Solomani parents.
- Mercier realized some people were being altered during normal medical procedures or over time.
- The alterations are gradual and evade quick scans and routine blood tests.
- High-level sequencing for elite status gates catches the changes.
- Mercier suspected the changes were being prepared as blackmail against elite families and officials.
- Mercier collected an initial physical sample from the President of Jardin's newborn son and planned to compare it after about two years.
- Those paired child samples became the proof hidden in Mercier's house.

### Psyker Mechanism

- Mercier connected the alteration method to research into creating psykers.
- The conspirators appear to hard-clone known psykers into children.
- About one in six such clones is likely to become a psyker.
- These psykers can use a new ability to mentally alter another person's body.
- The process is slow, cancerous, and cellular: marrow and white blood cell changes spread through the body.
- Within seven years, the victim may be wholly converted to the altered genetic pattern.
- This supports the possibility that the party's weapons were manipulated psionically during the [[Mercier Assassination]].
