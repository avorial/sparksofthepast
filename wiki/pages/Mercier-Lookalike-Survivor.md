# Mercier Lookalike Survivor

## Overview

The Mercier Lookalike Survivor is the only living person recovered from the sealed habitat at [[Hadal Research Site]]. He wears a Jardin Navy uniform and strongly resembles assassinated Genetics Minister [[Alain Mercier]].

## Session 4

The party rescues four survivors from the sealed chamber, but three die after transfer. This survivor is stabilized at [[Undersea Base Elyon]] after emergency treatment with nanites.

[[Varian Svazz (Allen)]] first notices the resemblance. Once the survivor is stabilized, the rest of the party also recognizes the connection.

## SolSec Interest

Saichi observes a [[SolSec Operative at Elyon]] entering the survivor's room and taking scans. Emma recognizes the likely purpose: SolSec can compare hand structure, fingerprints, bone-growth patterns, and genetic identity to test whether a person is a duplicate, clone, altered body, or genuine relative.

## Open Questions

- Is he Alain Mercier's twin, clone, relative, double, or something stranger?
- Why was he with Jardin Navy personnel at a Transstar site?
- Was Mercier's assassination connected to this undersea operation?
