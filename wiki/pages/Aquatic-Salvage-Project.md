# Aquatic Salvage Project

## Overview

The Aquatic Salvage Project is the party's first Transstar assignment after [[Session 3 - Transstar Day]]. [[Claire Bouchard]] assigns the group to the project during onboarding.

## Known Details

- Work begins at 4 a.m. after orientation.
- The project involves aquatic or underwater salvage.
- Transstar expects the group to use a submersible with a layout similar to a far trader, but without jump drives.
- The assignment needs loaders, spacecraft-adjacent crew, executive oversight, and armed support.
- Transstar describes the opposition as a rogue group of [[Dolphin Extremists]] interfering with salvage techniques.

## Relevance

The project may connect to earlier leads about the [[Sunken Ship-Boat]], though the exact relationship is not yet confirmed. Emma's executive role may allow the party to divert resources toward their own investigation while appearing to serve Transstar's needs.

## Open Questions

- What is Transstar salvaging?
- Why are dolphins opposing the work?
- Is the salvage operation legal, ecological, military, or covert?
- Can the party use the assignment to find or repair the sunken ship-boat?
