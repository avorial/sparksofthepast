# Krypton

## Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain

[[Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain]]

The material previously discussed is named [[GL15]] by Bellinger. She explains that substitute delivery methods damage RNA pathways and cause new failures even when prediction identifies the original problem. The crew neither visits Krypton nor acquires a physical GL15 sample in this session. The Imperial pleasure-cruiser lead remains unresolved.

## Session 8 - The Price of Clearing Your Name

[[Session 8 - The Price of Clearing Your Name]]

Beaumont says less than seven percent of the original stabilizing-material supply remains. Excavation waste from moon-habitat construction first drew scientific interest, followed unusually soon by an unnamed Imperial pleasure cruiser asking to inspect it. Its purpose remains unknown. Military passage toward Krypton is offered but not booked. The stellar readout F8V conflicts with the verbal description of a white dwarf; the inconsistency is unresolved.

In [[Session 7 - Echoes Beneath the Sea]], [[Francis Adams]] describes Krypton as a planet roughly four jumps north. An old Solomani site on one of its moons yielded the expendable resource used for perfect cloning. Records link shipments to Jardin and the [[Clone Rebirth Program]]. Storehouses are shrinking, encouraging experiments using less material and producing impaired clones.

The moon, site, material, suppliers, and exact route remain unnamed. The red-sun exchange is presented amid table humor, so it is not treated here as a verified astronomical specification. This is a lead from Francis, not a place the party has visited.
