# Deep-Trench Minisub

[[Transstar]] supplies this reinforced eight-person craft aboard [[TSS Profondeur]] for [[Session 7 - Echoes Beneath the Sea]]. It can descend farther than Profondeur and is deployed from the lower observation-nose area. It has two manipulator arms and an attached scouting drone, but no installed guns or torpedoes. Mining explosives are provided for the mission.

[[Dan Araujo (Josh)]] pilots the initial descent, scrapes a wall, then evades a patrol. [[Victor Mendes (Steinbach)]] runs sensors, [[Emma Colbert (Laura)]] communications, and [[Cass Pien (Kolb)]] assists through leadership. Dan later operates the drone. [[Varian Svazz (Allen)]] takes over piloting, secures the sub at the cave using an arm, and flies the successful escape.

Creaking and moisture occur at depth. A pressure transition displaces water and moves the sub toward the outer cave. No completed major repair is recorded. Navigation-log concealment is proposed but not confirmed. The vessel returns to Profondeur; it is borrowed corporate equipment, not a newly acquired party ship.
