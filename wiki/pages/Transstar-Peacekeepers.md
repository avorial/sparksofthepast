# Transstar Peacekeepers

## Overview

The Transstar Peacekeepers are the corporate police force seen inside the [[Transstar Sector Exchange]] during [[Session 3 - Transstar Day]]. Their uniforms differ from Jardin police and signal that the Exchange is governed by corporate law rather than normal planetary enforcement.

## Role

Inside Exchange territory, the Peacekeepers enforce Transstar rules, corporate contracts, commercial order, and internal security. Their authority is one of the reasons the party considers the Exchange a viable refuge while the [[Identity Veils]] fail.
