# Psychic Clone Conspiracy

## Overview

The Psychic Clone Conspiracy is the party's current model for the hidden force behind the [[Mercier Assassination]], the [[Mercier Frame-Up]], and the genetic anomalies in [[Mercier Genetic Findings]]. In [[Session 6 - Ghosts in the Bloodline]], [[Alain Mercier]]'s pad and research connect the mystery to a hidden subset of [[Social Solidarity]] capable of creating or exploiting psykers.

## What Mercier Found

Mercier discovered that important people were being genetically changed over time. The changes were not present from birth in every case and were not visible on ordinary scans. They only appeared during full sequencing for high-status life gates such as elite university admission, elected office, or military command.

The first clear trigger was a chancellor's son whose genetic code came back unacceptable despite being born from two supposedly ideal Solomani parents. Mercier then began preserving physical samples, including paired samples connected to the President of Jardin's child, to prove active alteration rather than bad heredity.

## Suspected Organization

Mercier believed a subset of Social Solidarity was running the program. The exact faction, leader, and chain of command are not yet confirmed. The group may sit inside official Social Solidarity structures, may have captured part of them, or may be using the ministry as cover.

## Psyker Production

The research suggests the faction hard-clones known psykers into children. Roughly one in six such clones is likely to become a psyker. This gives the faction a way to create more psionic assets and choose who has the chance to develop abilities.

## Genetic Manipulation Method

The psykers appear to have developed a new power that can alter another person's body through extreme mental effort. The transcript describes it as a kind of mental CRISPR or micromanipulation.

The process is slow and biological:

- A victim is exposed to the psionic alteration.
- White blood cells or bone marrow begin to change.
- As marrow replaces cells over time, 30% to 40% of the body may carry the alteration.
- Within about seven years, the person may be wholly converted to the altered pattern.

## Political Purpose

The most likely purpose is blackmail and control. Jardin's elites depend on genetic purity, status records, and full sequencing for legitimacy. If a political heir, military commander, or senior official suddenly tests unacceptable, the scandal can end a family or career.

The conspiracy can use that threat to make targets comply, remove rivals, or prevent officials from challenging them.

## Connection to the Frame-Up

The party now has a plausible explanation for the impossible weapon behavior during the [[Mercier Assassination]]. If psykers were trying to stop Mercier from exposing a takeover or blackmail program, they may have manipulated minds, weapons, shots, or perception in ways ordinary evidence could not explain.

This does not clear the party publicly yet, but it gives them a theory that fits the frame-up.
