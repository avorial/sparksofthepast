# Snowball

The merchant ship Snowball leaves a distress probe and hides in [[Free Arden]]'s atmosphere to avoid a much stronger pirate/mercenary vessel. In [[Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain]], the crew decodes the probe and forwards its genuine call for help while posing as a SolSec advance vessel.

[[HMS Hank Aaron]] arrives and destroys the pursuer. Snowball thanks the Travellers and heads toward Jardin, visibly damaged and leaking hydrogen after twenty-two hours fighting atmospheric winds. The GM says it can make the trip; possible jump-system trouble is a concern rather than a diagnosed failure.

The crew does not board, salvage, repair, or receive a stated reward from Snowball. Its captain and final arrival are not identified.
