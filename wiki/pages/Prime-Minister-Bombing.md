# Prime Minister Bombing

## Overview

During [[Session 5 - The Oduya]], news reports that the Prime Minister's home was bombed that morning. The broadcast describes the attack as suspected separatist terrorism and presumes the Prime Minister dead.

The party recognizes the residence as the same place where the group previously delivered Lucin's sealed crate to [[Chief Helene Marchand]].

## Why It Matters

The bombing occurs shortly after [[Alain Mercier]] explains that the destruction of [[Hadal Research Site]] may have been a preparatory strike against clone backups. If a high-ranking political figure had a prepared clone body at Hadal, destroying that backup first would make a later assassination permanent.

## Open Questions

- Was the Prime Minister actually killed?
- Did the Prime Minister have a clone backup at Hadal?
- Are suspected separatists responsible, or are they another cover story like the [[Mercier Frame-Up]]?
- Did the bombing target the person, the residence, the staff, or records inside?
