# Anita Singh

## Overview

Anita Singh is one of [[Emma Colbert (Laura)]]'s contacts. She is a sharp-witted entrepreneur whose logistics consortium quietly controls warehousing, orbital freight handling, and labor contracts across several Confederation starports.

## Session 5

During [[Session 5 - The Oduya]], Emma identifies Anita as a viable channel for fencing the illegal cybernetic cargo recovered from [[Oduya]]. Rather than simply paying cash, Anita can trade value into ship repair services, plates, replacement monitors, cleanup, and refueling support.

## Relevance

Anita gives the party a way to turn dangerous contraband into practical repairs without presenting wanted fugitives at a normal market.
