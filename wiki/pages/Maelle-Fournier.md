# Maëlle Fournier

![Maëlle Fournier portrait](/wiki/media/Maelle-Fournier.png)

*Engineer / Damage Control*

## Overview

Maëlle Fournier is the engineer and damage-control specialist aboard the [[TSS Profondeur]]. She is former Jardin Navy, experienced with the vessel's pipes, repairs, sensors, and combat damage.

She introduces herself carrying a sensor in one hand and a welding tool in the other, making clear that anyone aboard the Profondeur needs to be useful rather than merely taking up air.

## Session 4

Fournier helps orient the party toward shipboard repair expectations and the reality of undersea salvage work. Her role frames the technical side of the Hadal mission: pressure, seals, welding, power, and keeping old systems alive long enough to survive.
