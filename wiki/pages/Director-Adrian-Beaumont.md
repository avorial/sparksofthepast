# Director Adrian Beaumont

## Role

In [[Session 8 - The Price of Clearing Your Name]], Beaumont meets the party on [[Providence]] after tracking [[Mercier's Locked Pad]]. He describes [[Alain Mercier]] as his agent, and says a major faction of [[Social Solidarity]] and a smaller group inside [[SolSec]] are exploiting replication and psychic abilities for political control. He is a separate NPC from [[Director Luc Beaumont]], the Port Authority director on Jardin.

## Position and Claims

Beaumont's investigative network is failing. He has left a demotion notice unopened to delay formal acknowledgment while loyal agents have already checked its contents. He expects loss of authority within days, likely enforced retirement, and possible interrogation rather than certain immediate execution.

He alleges 35-40 percent of Social Solidarity belongs to the splinter group. He says [[Camille Dufour]] was a defector, ordered her killed after she left Providence, and first removed her replication eligibility so no replacement would activate. The party hears his account but does not independently investigate every claim.

## Assistance

He gives the crew Cr1,000,000, a key to the [[North Meridian Intelligence Vault]], temporary military credentials, an emergency high-level Social Solidarity transponder identity, and an intermediary communication method. His plan requires personnel-continuity records, the Heredity Register, and evidence from the altered-genetics laboratory. He hopes these can persuade Jardin's president to clear the crew and investigate the faction.

He offers military passage toward [[Krypton]] rather than a ship refit that would leave the crew stationary and vulnerable. He agrees to a possible recruitment-cover story about Zoy's psychic potential; this is fabrication, not evidence of a psychic ability.

## Krypton Information

He reports less than seven percent of the original stabilizing-material supply remains, and describes a historical Imperial pleasure cruiser that inspected excavation waste unusually soon after the first report. The cruiser is unnamed. He also clarifies the role of accelerated aging in adult clones' reduced lifespan.

## Last Seen

Saichi detects an attacker's targeting light; Beaumont takes cover. After her fire detonates the attacker's rocket launcher, [[Lieutenant Baxter]] moves to protect him. Beaumont survives the immediate encounter; his subsequent fate is unknown.
