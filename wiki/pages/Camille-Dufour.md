# Camille Dufour

## Session 8 - The Price of Clearing Your Name

[[Session 8 - The Price of Clearing Your Name]]

[[Director Adrian Beaumont]] alleges Camille was a SolSec defector helping distort Mercier's investigation and arrange a controlled replacement. He says he removed her A7 eligibility and ordered her killed as she left Providence. The GM clarifies her backup was destroyed at the facility associated with Mercier's rescue and is not in the hidden community. The party does not meet her alive or independently examine her death; earlier assumptions of her loyal mission are superseded by this attributed account.

## Overview

Camille Dufour is a SolSec agent identified from the flight record tied to [[Mercier's Locked Pad]] in [[Session 6 - Ghosts in the Bloodline]]. Her ID is encrypted, and the recovered record includes a photo of a woman in a black uniform.

## Session 6 Discovery

Mercier's pad showed a used off-world ticket to [[Providence]]. The ticket had expired, but the registry showed it had been used. The party first wondered whether someone had taken Mercier's flight, but the pad and follow-up investigation show Mercier bought the ticket for someone else.

[[Emma Colbert (Laura)]] contacted [[Mark Duncan]], a compartmentalized travel-clearance contact, to identify the passenger. Mark returned a record and photo naming Camille Dufour as the traveler.

## Known Role

- SolSec agent with encrypted identity details.
- Wore or was photographed in a black uniform.
- Trusted enough by [[Alain Mercier]] to be sent ahead in a secretive capacity.
- Traveled to [[Providence]] shortly before Mercier's public assassination.
- Intended to confront or interrogate a Social Solidarity executive about Mercier's allegations.
