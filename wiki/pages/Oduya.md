# Oduya

## Overview

Oduya is the true name of the [[Sunken Ship-Boat]] raised by the party during [[Session 5 - The Oduya]]. The two names refer to the same craft: "Sunken Ship-Boat" was the old lead, and Oduya is the identified vessel.

It is a small craft capable of reaching orbit and other planets in the system, but it has no jump drive.

The name means "openness." The craft had spent roughly forty years underwater before the party found it.

## Condition Found

- The hull was pressure-damaged and partly buried in silt and sea growth.
- Four crew were dead aboard, including a captain found in a locker.
- One crew member was an uplifted gorilla.
- The bridge or pilot compartment suffered a pressure blowout.
- Reactor leakage once made the wreck dangerous enough that official investigators avoided full recovery.
- The craft carried removable false markings rather than painted permanent Navy identification.

## Recovery

[[0-31 Saichi Maru (Annette)]] reached the wreck first, documented the exterior, recorded the dead crew, and spotted the removable markings. The rest of the party arrived with the [[TSS Profondeur]], patched the hull, restored partial power, drained water, booted the computer, and got the craft moving.

The Profondeur briefly acted as a decoy when a small unresponsive craft approached the salvage site.

## Logs

Oduya was a smuggling craft doing business with the [[Jardin Syndicate]] and dolphin contacts. It carried goods for underwater pickup and changed its apparent identity through removable transponders and exterior wraps.

The ship was lost after a Navy patrol caught it during a drop. Oduya dove to escape the surface craft, but the patrol used depth charges. The pressure shock injured or killed the crew before they could recover.

## Cargo Recovered

- Common raw materials, mostly corroded or low-value.
- Autorifles destroyed by water exposure.
- Illegal cybernetic weapons and implants.
- A valuable living tree in a coded hibernation chamber.
- The [[Transponder Swap Box]] held by the dead captain.

## Use to the Party

Once repaired, Oduya can move [[Alain Mercier]] off-world or into orbit without returning him to the surface. It also gives the party a system-scale craft with changeable identities, useful for reaching other planets before they acquire a jump-capable ship.
