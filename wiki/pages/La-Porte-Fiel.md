# La Porte Fiel

## Overview

La Porte Fiel is a club or drug den in [[Quartier-Sainte-Liberte]]. The party found [[Lucin]] there during [[Session 3 - Transstar Day]] after spotting another trike like the one Lucin had given them.

## Description

The entrance is watched by a heavily cybered bouncer. Inside, the room is dominated by bass-heavy music, flashing lights, reclining chairs, and patrons using inhaled drug packets. Most people are seated, leaning against the bar, or too altered to pay attention.

Lucin used the back area, where gas canisters and packet-filling equipment were visible, as a private space for business.

## Session 3

The party arranged for Lucin to deliver the recovered Mercier evidence to [[Inspector Marc Valois]]. During the handoff, [[Mercier's Locked Pad]] activated its tracking signal. Lucin sealed the pad in a Faraday bag, and the group fled before police arrived at the den.
