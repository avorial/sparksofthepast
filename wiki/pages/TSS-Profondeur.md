# TSS Profondeur

## Overview

The TSS Profondeur is a Transstar salvage submersible assigned to the [[Aquatic Salvage Project]]. It was formerly a Jardin Navy vessel, damaged during prior service and later purchased or transferred to [[Transstar]] because full naval repair was not worth the expense.

[[Étienne Valcourt]] commands the vessel. The senior crew includes [[Maëlle Fournier]] for engineering and damage control, [[Rémi Beauchamp]] for sonar, sensors, and communications, and [[Sabine Caron]] for security and diving operations.

## Known Details

- The vessel is around twenty-five to thirty years old.
- It can travel faster than orcas, but full-speed operation is noisy enough to advertise its position on sonar.
- Its missile room has been converted into pressurized rescue tubes or escape pods.
- The ship is under-crewed because experienced hands were moved to train another vessel.
- Hidden energy weapons are stored aboard but logged as welders.
- Prior salvage logs mention equipment, oxygenators, power couplers, and scrubbed rescue details.
- Food service is processed cubes with spice packets; chicken cacciatore and Szechuan sauce are crew favorites.

## Session 4

The party boards the Profondeur for its first Transstar shift. The crew trains them in shipboard stations, undersea suits, drone work, and emergency procedures before traveling to [[Hadal Research Site]].

After the Hadal rescue, the Profondeur diverts to [[Undersea Base Elyon]] to save the surviving Jardin Navy officer.
