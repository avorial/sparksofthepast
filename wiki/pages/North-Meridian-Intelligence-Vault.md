# North Meridian Intelligence Vault

## Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain

[[Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain]]

The crew still holds the Game 8 personnel and additional dossiers. Game 9 uses another source to investigate research personnel; no full on-screen decoding of every vault case or confirmed acquisition of the separate Heredity Register occurs. Bellinger's testimony/research is the new laboratory-evidence lead.

## Access

During [[Session 8 - The Price of Clearing Your Name]], the Travellers enter the military base on [[North Meridian]] using temporary credentials and inspection cover. [[Lieutenant Colonel Pablo Garcia]] provides escorts. At the vault, [[Director Adrian Beaumont]]'s access key answers an optical challenge; automated defenses power down and the escorts remain outside.

A terminal and illuminated floor panels direct users to requested holdings. Victor adopts a guard position while Zoy operates the terminal with leadership assistance.

## Recovered Records

- Personnel-continuity data identifies SolSec/Social Solidarity operatives, their undercover networks, and information described as useful for the next ninety days. Testing histories can guide investigation of the splinter faction.
- Additional options cover the president and vice-president and SolSec's intelligence on the dolphin underground network.
- Despite Emma's objections, Zoy chooses all offered categories.
- The archive produces copies on briefcase-sized storage units, too large for a personal pad. The originals remain in the vault.
- The transcript does not settle the exact number of physical cases or whether the two political leaders' files occupy separate units.

## Limits

The records have not yet been fully analyzed when the session ends. The Heredity Register and biological laboratory evidence remain separate objectives. A possible audit trail and the consequences of copying extra dossiers are unresolved. Victor estimates eight to sixteen hours before the base reports the visit through its next outbound update; this is not a guaranteed safe window.
