# Session 8 - The Price of Clearing Your Name

## Synopsis

With its workweek complete and its repaired ship-boat ready, the crew finally has freedom to travel around the Jardin system. Their [[Transstar]] employment still provides cover, and they initially intend to return before three days of leave expire. [[Oduya|Volante]] is fueled and spaceworthy but still smells, requires constant attention at the controls, carries no installed ship weapons, and has no jump drive. [[Emma Colbert (Laura)]] brokers a small contract that should cover a week's fuel and maintenance, with possible pocket money after returning to Jardin. The crew chooses [[Providence]], hoping to find [[Camille Dufour]] and advance the investigation that could clear their names.

[[Varian Svazz (Allen)]] gets them into orbit. [[Victor Mendes (Steinbach)]] plots an excellent slingshot course, reducing the three-AU journey to about seven hours. [[Dan Araujo (Josh)]] carefully manages the power system instead of pushing it. En route, the crew identifies a comet and an unreflective dark rock as possible mining targets for a later return trip. Emma privately checks the genetic identities copied from the [[Hidden Clone Community]] against active records and finds that about ten percent still have another version alive. Nobody is evacuated and no mining occurs.

Providence is a manicured garden world of retreats, scientific institutions, senior officials, and SolSec training facilities, sharply different from Jardin's urban sprawl. Its authorities politely direct the small craft to a landing bay, while two gunships provide a routine security escort. Varian declines paid automated landing and successfully brings the ship into a tight, tree-lined berth. Service robots swarm the port, but their presence does not prove they are conscious. Emma notices an unusual silence in her implanted communications.

Using [[Mercier's Locked Pad]], the party approaches the office where Mercier's meeting was scheduled. About ten SolSec agents surround the courtyard. An agent fires a warning shot, prompting [[0-31 Saichi Maru (Annette)]] to deploy her weapon and enter protective mode. The leader holsters his own pistol, allowing a tense de-escalation. He introduces himself as [[Lieutenant Baxter]], a member of [[Director Adrian Beaumont]]'s private security team. Beaumont has been tracking Mercier's pad and wants to speak to its carriers. An autonomous vehicle brings the group to his offices.

Over breakfast refreshments, Beaumont explains that the official replacement program is a state secret distinct from the conspiracy Mercier was investigating. He alleges that an extremist faction accounting for perhaps 35-40 percent of [[Social Solidarity]] has been altering bloodlines and creating psychic replacements, and has recruited a smaller but important group inside [[SolSec]]. He claims Camille was one of those defectors, intended to help substitute a controlled replacement for Mercier, and was killed on Beaumont's orders after her departure from Providence. He first removed her replacement eligibility so no backup would be activated.

Beaumont's own position is collapsing. Loyal agents have inspected a notice he has deliberately left unopened; acknowledging it will formally remove his rank and investigative powers. He gives the Travellers a route to exposing the faction: obtain personnel-continuity records, a hereditary reference register, and evidence from the laboratory manufacturing the altered genetic material. He hopes the combined evidence can persuade Jardin's president to clear their names and order investigations. For the first step, he supplies an access key to the [[North Meridian Intelligence Vault]], temporary military credentials, an emergency high-level Social Solidarity transponder identity, an intermediary contact method, and Cr1,000,000.

Before the meeting can end safely, Saichi spots a targeting light through a gap in the window. She warns everyone to take cover, then fires toward its source. The successful shot triggers an explosion in the neighboring building: the attacker was carrying a rocket launcher. Baxter moves to protect Beaumont while the party escapes with the aid of a guide drone. Varian and Dan get Volante into the air; Varian uses another departing ship-boat as a distraction while security gunships pursue the wrong vessel.

Rather than return immediately to work, the crew takes its new opportunity and heads for [[North Meridian]]. Victor's exceptional astrogation cuts the next passage from about seven hours to two. The volcanic world has an unbreathable atmosphere and a guarded military installation. With suit checks completed, the Travellers use their temporary identities and claim to be conducting a surprise inspection. [[Lieutenant Colonel Pablo Garcia]] accepts the visit and provides an escort. At the vault, Beaumont's key disables the automated defenses and admits the party without its guards.

[[Zoy (Damon)]] operates the vault terminal with leadership support. The system produces a briefcase-sized copy of the personnel and undercover-network records, described as useful over the next ninety days. It also offers records on the president and vice-president, plus intelligence on the dolphin criminal network. Despite Emma's emphatic objections to taking information beyond their mission, Zoy chooses all the offered categories. The group leaves with multiple large data units and departs the base without a fight. Their next destination is [[Domirina]], called Dorma in the recording, a biological research site on a moon of [[Free Arden]]. The flight is expected to take eighteen to twenty hours across roughly nine AU, giving them time to examine the data next game.

### What Beaumont Says About the Conspiracy

These are Beaumont's statements and the GM's clarifications during the meeting. His ideological descriptions of genetic purity are his worldview, not neutral facts about a person's worth.

- The official program preserves high-status people after violent death and is intended to provide replacements, not openly multiply living individuals.

- Mercier already knew about official replication. His investigation focused on deliberate alteration of living people's genetic markers without conventional procedures.
- Beaumont alleges that around 35-40 percent of Social Solidarity belongs to the splinter faction, and a smaller prominent group of SolSec agents has joined it.
- He believes key faction leaders are psychics created through replication experiments and genetic alteration, rather than an unrelated psychic organization infiltrating from outside.
- He identifies Mercier's planned disclosure and demand for retesting as the reason Mercier was killed. He cites psychic involvement as evidence against the faction; the party does not independently resolve the entire assassination here.
- He calls Mercier an agent of his. The transcript sometimes says Adrian Mercer; existing campaign identity remains [[Alain Mercier]]. No second Mercier NPC is created.

### The Three Evidence Objectives

1. **Personnel continuity:** North Meridian holds identities and continuity/testing histories for security personnel, including covert agents and their civilian covers. Cross-reference the records with genetic-test timing to identify suspicious gaps. An old test is an investigative lead, not automatic proof of guilt.
2. **Heredity Register:** A reference set described as approximately 185 core Solomani genetic markers, with an immediately looser reference to 180. Beaumont requests that this information not be published. It would enable comparison against altered samples. Acquisition is not confirmed this session.
3. **Laboratory evidence:** Locate the facility manufacturing or introducing the altered genetic material and obtain its records. Beaumont mentions North Meridian, Casron, or Terra Nova's moons as possibilities; by departure the party chooses Domirina as its next biological-research lead. The correct lab is not yet proven.

Beaumont expects all three to make a compelling case to [[Le Palais de Continuité|Jardin's president]]. GM clarification suggests even the first could materially reduce government pursuit, but no pardon or exoneration occurs in Game 8.

## Podcast Description

With three days of leave and a battered ship-boat, the crew heads to Providence in search of the truth behind Mercier's murder. A tense meeting with a threatened SolSec director exposes a psychic conspiracy within the government and offers a dangerous path to clearing their names, until a targeting beam turns breakfast into an assassination attempt. Escaping with a million credits, military credentials, and a key to a heavily guarded vault, the Travellers bluff their way into North Meridian's secrets. But when the chance comes to take more information than they were sent for, the crew discovers that agreeing on how much truth they want may be harder than getting past the guards.
