# Crew of TSS Profondeur

## Public Face

The crew of the [[TSS Profondeur]] is the small Transstar team assigned to run the party's first [[Aquatic Salvage Project]] shift. They are experienced, practical, and used to undersea operations, but the vessel is notably under-crewed after experienced hands were shifted elsewhere.

- [[Etienne Valcourt]] - captain and pilot.
- [[Maelle Fournier]] - engineer and damage-control specialist.
- [[Remi Beauchamp]] - sonar, sensors, electronics, and communications specialist.
- [[Sabine Caron]] - security and diving-operations lead.

## Relationships

The crew treats the party as temporary Transstar workers until the group proves useful during the [[Hadal Research Site]] rescue. Valcourt especially respects the successful rescue attempt, even though the result creates a serious political and legal problem.

During [[Session 5 - The Oduya]], Valcourt accepts Emma's confidential argument that helping the party reach the [[Sunken Ship-Boat]] may serve Transstar's interests and solve the restored [[Alain Mercier]] problem. The Profondeur later supports the recovery of [[Oduya]] by delivering the party, supplying oxygen, and drawing away an approaching craft.
