# Institut de Continuité Biologique

The crew infiltrates this installation on [[Domirina]], the second research moon of [[Free Arden]], in [[Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain]]. The original NPC reference names it Institut de Continuité Biologique. Free Arden Research Facility remains an alias and the existing page address. Its entry interface displays JRS station security.

## Layout and Entry

Solar panels cover about a quarter of the surface, with a central ship-supplies shaft and a service/personnel elevator. The party lands in a crater, hides beneath tilting arrays, and uses the service entry. An automated non-biological lost-pass workflow admits Saichi. Technicians' genetic access and RFID tags subsequently admit the biological crew.

An argon layer separates the surface approach from the oxygenated interior in the game's environmental description. Inside are a repair/drone bay, emergency suits, staff quarters, sequencing laboratories, incineration facilities, and containment sections with measures intended to limit psychic interference. The primary crew suits have six-hour supplies; emergency suits offer only about forty-five to sixty minutes.

## Research and Personnel

[[Dr. Élodie Vasseur]] directs research and [[Commandant Rémi Leduc]] oversees security. [[Claire Montfort]] handles administration. [[Dr. Anaïs Bellanger]] develops predictive genetic models and holds sensitive patterns in her eidetic memory. [[Sabine Delacroix]] is a documented psychic trainer; [[Mireille Fournier]] tends subjects and [[Nicolas Perrin]] handles environmental systems.

Records establish that deadline-driven researchers create short-lived accelerated self-copies and dispose of their bodies. GL15 scarcity creates further pressure. Bellinger says psychic agents use models read from her mind to manipulate political families.

## Raid Outcome

The complete original cast and portrait directory is retained in the GM section below.

The party kills two technicians, inserts three staff covers, extracts Bellinger, and leaves with her locked research and claimed recording of target instructions. She releases containment to distract security, causing gunfire and explosions. Saichi incapacitates an armed defender and the other combatant passes through the floor. The facility's final survival, casualty count, and fate of released subjects are unknown. No full GL15 stockpile is recovered.
