# Hidden Clone Community

## Overview

Discovered in [[Session 7 - Echoes Beneath the Sea]], this unnamed settlement lies beyond the middle of three cave entrances in the [[Crushed Trench]]. About 250-300 residents live here. Continuous databases suggest roughly 25-30 years of occupation. The inhabitants are discarded backups and restored clones from the [[Clone Rebirth Program]], not collectively a military terrorist cell.

## Facilities and Access

A mechanical two-door airlock leads from an underwater cave and air pocket into dry inhabited space. Treated rock contains the pressure transition. Repair bays, oxygen-cylinder exchanges, metal stairs, powered wall-side lifts, a cafe, a lagoon used for drinking water, and computer learning facilities support daily life. Civilian computers are repurposed medical terminals. Surface broadcasts are relayed rather than connected directly; [[Francis Adams]] describes a changing source retransmitting about every six hours.

## Residents

Most residents wear heavily repaired work or laboratory clothes, often stripped of Transstar insignia. Weapons are rare. Children are about one in sixty residents, with no teenagers observed. No uplifted apes are seen. Dolphins can move through the dry city in mechanical walking rigs. Francis works as a psychologist. A younger counterpart of [[Judith Dumont (Carla)]] is seen talking to him; she is a separate person from the player character.

## Economy and Danger

Old bank accounts, programming, intelligence analysis, smuggling knowledge, and criminal contacts sustain the settlement. Residents pay rescuers to recover clones due for disposal. They believe this buys rescue, while a separate armed faction also seeks information from clone memories. [[Transstar]] does not know this city exists and assumes stolen clones could not survive. [[Social Solidarity]] has tried to trace residents who contact family under known identities.

Some residents have escaped, but most fear detection and destruction on the surface. Criminal visitors sometimes abduct people. No reliable evacuation network is established.

## Game 7 Outcome

The party conceals its visit, downloads genetic data, and bombs a different cave instead of harming the city. A proposal to obscure minisub logs is recorded, but its completion is unconfirmed. No evacuation or confirmed warning to the residents occurs. The location remains unnamed in the source; this page title is descriptive.
