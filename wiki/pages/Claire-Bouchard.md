# Claire Bouchard

*Highport Operations Manager*

## Overview

Claire Bouchard manages Highport operations for [[Transstar]] at Port Sainte-Jeanne, coordinating thousands of arrivals and departures every week. She has a reputation for absolute efficiency: nothing surprises her, nothing delays her, and nothing intimidates her. She can reroute half a sector's shipping traffic while eating lunch, which makes her one of the most quietly indispensable people in Jardin orbit.

## Session 3

Claire handled the party's onboarding after the aptitude tests in [[Session 3 - Transstar Day]]. She gathered the newly hired group in an HR-style room and thanked them for joining [[Transstar]]. Because the system had difficulty reading the party's veiled faces at first, she had them complete a questionnaire and then issued employee badges based on the real faces captured during VR testing.

She explained the thirty-day open employment window, the later two-, four-, or six-year term options, salary deductions for housing and food, and the practical protection of Transstar employment inside the [[Transstar Sector Exchange]].

Claire assigned the group to the [[Aquatic Salvage Project]] and allowed them to stay together in Emma's executive suite on floor 162 rather than separate housing blocks.
