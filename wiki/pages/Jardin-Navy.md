# Jardin Navy

## Overview

The Jardin Navy is the planetary naval force operating from facilities such as [[Fort Robespierre Naval Annex]]. It becomes newly important in [[Session 4 - Into the Hadal Deep]] when the party finds Jardin Navy personnel trapped inside a Transstar undersea research facility.

## Session 4

At [[Hadal Research Site]], the party recovers four starving survivors and ten dead personnel from a sealed oxygenated chamber. All are in Jardin Navy uniforms. Only one survivor lives after transfer to [[Undersea Base Elyon]]: the [[Mercier Lookalike Survivor]].

[[Étienne Valcourt]] points out the contradiction. Hadal is a Transstar site, so a full naval group being trapped there suggests a covert operation, a failed rescue, or an official story that does not match what happened.

## Open Questions

- Why was a naval team at Hadal?
- Where is the ship or transport that brought them there?
