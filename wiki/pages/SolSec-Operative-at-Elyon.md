# SolSec Operative at Elyon

## Overview

The SolSec Operative at Elyon is an unidentified agent seen inside [[Undersea Base Elyon]] during [[Session 4 - Into the Hadal Deep]]. Saichi spots him entering the [[Mercier Lookalike Survivor]]'s room late at night.

## Session 4

The operative scans the survivor's hand and bone structure. Emma recognizes the likely significance: SolSec is testing whether the survivor is genetically identical, physically altered, cloned, or otherwise connected to [[Alain Mercier]].

Zoy and Ciernan attempt to intercept the operative afterward. The agent quickly draws a gun on Zoy, identifies the party as enemies of the state, warns them not to interfere with SolSec activity, and leaves without escalating further.

## Session 5

The party debriefs the confrontation and decides the operative is too dangerous to approach casually. After [[Alain Mercier]] wakes and explains the [[Clone Rebirth Program]], the operative's scan reads as an attempt to determine whether Mercier is a restored clone, an original, or part of a clone-fraud scenario.
