# Reena Kessler

## Overview

Reena Kessler works an entry booth at the [[Transstar Sector Exchange]]. During [[Session 3 - Transstar Day]], she greeted the first party members entering the Exchange and asked whether they were there to shop or seek work.

## Session 3

Reena issued three-day holographic Transstar work-seeker stamps. She explained that visitors can use the pass to seek employment, file resumes, and explore their fit inside the Transstar organization. If they find work, the temporary pass can become a permanent Transstar ID.

She also explained that Transstar employees receive corporate discounts and that employment changes a person's legal relationship to Jardin: the employee becomes part of the Transstar corporate system rather than an ordinary planetary citizen.
