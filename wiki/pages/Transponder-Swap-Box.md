# Transponder Swap Box

## Overview

The Transponder Swap Box is a metallic case recovered by [[0-31 Saichi Maru (Annette)]] from the dead captain of [[Oduya]] during [[Session 5 - The Oduya]]. The case contains multiple cylindrical transponder modules, each built from subtly different marked metals.

## Function

The modules are removable ship transponders. Oduya has an internal plate that accepts one module, draws it deep into the computer, replaces the active identity, and ejects the previous transponder.

The system explains why the wreck carried large removable exterior markings: Oduya could alter both its broadcast identity and visible nationality.

## Known Identities

The recovered set includes five usable changes in addition to whatever is currently installed:

- Two Solomani transponders.
- One very old pre-war Solomani transponder.
- One Imperium transponder.
- One Aslan transponder.

## Relevance

For ordinary travel, ships broadcast identity constantly. This box gives the party a way to disguise Oduya on a planetary or system scale, slip past routine tracking, approach worlds under different profiles, or rescue contacts without immediately tying those actions to their known identities.

## Risks

- Any identity may have its own history, debts, enemies, or watchlist flags.
- Using an Imperium or Aslan transponder inside Solomani-controlled space could attract dangerous attention.
- The hardware is delicate and old; mishandling could damage the modules.
