# Transponder Swap Box

## Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain

[[Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain]]

The crew broadcasts as SolSec ship S42 while forwarding Snowball's genuine distress call. Victor's deception convinces Hank Aaron and supports the naval rescue. The team refrains from immediately changing identity under the carrier's observation. No exact later switch time, permanent immunity, or verification by naval intelligence is established.

## Session 8 - The Price of Clearing Your Name

[[Session 8 - The Price of Clearing Your Name]]

Beaumont knows the ship's identity-changing capability, attributing the information to an interrogated crash survivor who is not securely named. He supplies a short-lived emergency Social Solidarity identity for high-level government cover; it is not used in the played escape. Temporary personal military credentials are a separate asset.

## Overview

The Transponder Swap Box is a metallic case recovered by [[0-31 Saichi Maru (Annette)]] from the dead captain of [[Oduya]] during [[Session 5 - The Oduya]]. The case contains multiple cylindrical transponder modules, each built from subtly different marked metals.

## Function

The modules are removable ship transponders. Oduya has an internal plate that accepts one module, draws it deep into the computer, replaces the active identity, and ejects the previous transponder.

The system explains why the wreck carried large removable exterior markings: Oduya could alter both its broadcast identity and visible nationality.

## Known Identities

The recovered set includes five usable changes in addition to whatever is currently installed:

- Two Solomani transponders.
- One very old pre-war Solomani transponder.
- One Imperium transponder.
- One Aslan transponder.

## Relevance

For ordinary travel, ships broadcast identity constantly. This box gives the party a way to disguise Oduya on a planetary or system scale, slip past routine tracking, approach worlds under different profiles, or rescue contacts without immediately tying those actions to their known identities.

## Risks

- Any identity may have its own history, debts, enemies, or watchlist flags.
- Using an Imperium or Aslan transponder inside Solomani-controlled space could attract dangerous attention.
- The hardware is delicate and old; mishandling could damage the modules.

## Session 6 - Ghost Transponder Quirk

During [[Session 6 - Ghosts in the Bloodline]], the party learns that Oduya/Volante's transponder system has been hacked beyond simple module swapping. Because it has been modified for permanently linked transponders, a user can also attempt to code an identity on the fly with Electronics (Computers).

This gives +1 success to attempts to disguise the ship's identity, but it is risky. The system builds from registrations it has previously scanned, so it might broadcast a real ship's sister profile or another identity with unknown legal trouble attached.
