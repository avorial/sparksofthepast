# Mercier Penthouse

## Overview

Mercier Penthouse is [[Alain Mercier]]'s high-rise residence, accessed by personal code in [[Session 6 - Ghosts in the Bloodline]]. It is the location where Mercier hid the physical proof behind his genetic-alteration accusations.

## Layout Notes

- The residence is a penthouse suite in one of the towers.
- The elevator opens directly into the apartment rather than into a public hallway.
- The apartment was already being tossed when the party arrived.
- A secret door sits behind the television.

## Session 6 Raid

The away team consists of [[0-31 Saichi Maru (Annette)]], [[Cass Pien (Kolb)]], [[Dan Araujo (Josh)]], and [[Victor Mendes (Steinbach)]]. They use Mercier's code to ride the elevator up. When the doors open, they hear movement stop inside.

Saichi climbs up the elevator/wall/ceiling line using gecko grippers to draw fire away from the humans. Cass follows low. Apparent [[Home Guard]] personnel open fire with shotguns. Victor is nearly killed, Saichi is damaged, Cass is lightly wounded, and Dan saves Victor with immediate first aid.

After the fight, the team secures the bodies, comms, and cuffs before finding the hidden cache.

## Hidden Cache

The secret compartment contains [[Mercier Evidence Cache]]:

- Five physical genetic comparison samples.
- Research notes on how the alterations occur.
- Photos of suspected Social Solidarity conspirators.
- A hidden communicator.
- A laser pistol similar to Cass's.
- About Cr200,000.
