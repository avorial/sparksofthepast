# Mercier Evidence Cache

## Overview

The Mercier Evidence Cache is the hidden proof recovered from [[Mercier Penthouse]] during [[Session 6 - Ghosts in the Bloodline]]. Unlike copied files or rumor, it contains physical samples that can support [[Alain Mercier]]'s claim that important people are being genetically altered over time.

## Contents

- Five actual comparison samples from people whose genetic code changed over time.
- Blood drops on slides with associated readouts.
- Research explaining how Mercier thought the changes were occurring.
- Photos of people Mercier suspected were part of the Social Solidarity opposition/conspiracy group.
- A hidden communicator.
- A laser pistol similar to [[Cass Pien (Kolb)]]'s.
- About Cr200,000.

## Evidence Value

The samples matter because they are not just assertions from a dead or restored official. They are physical material that can be tested. The most important known example is tied to the President of Jardin's son: Mercier took an initial sample at birth and intended to compare it after roughly two years to prove an active change.

## Risks

- Anyone named or photographed in the cache may already be watching for it.
- The communicator could be useful or traceable.
- The physical samples cannot be trivially duplicated while preserving evidentiary value.
- Taking the cache makes the party more dangerous to the conspiracy and more valuable to anyone seeking leverage over Jardin.
