# Undersea Suits

## Overview

Undersea suits are pressure-rated suits used by Transstar crews during deep salvage and underwater combat. They use familiar vac-suit procedures and skill, but they are not ordinary space suits: they are built to survive high external pressure rather than vacuum.

## Session 4

[[Sabine Caron]] trains the party on suit use aboard the [[TSS Profondeur]]. The crew learns buddy checks, suit lights, communications channels, pressure concerns, and tank color-coding for different depths and gas mixes.

Tool users carry backpack repair kits. Security personnel can carry undersea weapons such as harpoon guns. The training emphasizes that ordinary slug weapons and careless laser fire can endanger a soft hull or suit environment.

## Known Details

- Buddy checks take longer but improve the chance of safe operation.
- Suit computers show distance to ship and teammates, but not always direction.
- Neck mics and private channels manage communications outside the ship.
- Exterior work can involve phantom heat/cold, pressure stress, claustrophobia, and heartbeat noise inside the suit.
