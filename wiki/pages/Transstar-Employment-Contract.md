# Transstar Employment Contract

## Overview

The Transstar employment contract is the legal shield the party accepts in [[Session 3 - Transstar Day]]. As employees, they are protected from ordinary Jardin prosecution while inside the [[Transstar Sector Exchange]] or aboard a Transstar ship.

## Terms Learned

- A three-day work-seeker stamp allows entry to seek employment.
- Successful applicants receive Transstar employee badges.
- The first thirty days are open employment: the employee may leave, and Transstar may fire them.
- After thirty days, the employee must choose a two-, four-, or six-year term if they stay.
- Housing, food, storage, cleaning, and other costs are deducted from pay before salary reaches the employee's badge or credit account.
- Personal anti-state beliefs are tolerated only if kept private and non-disruptive.

## Party Impact

The contract gives the party time after the [[Identity Veils]] fail. It also places their true faces in Transstar's system, binds them to corporate rules, and makes clearing their names harder to prioritize around work.
