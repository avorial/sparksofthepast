# Hadal Research Site

## Overview

Hadal Research Site is a damaged Transstar undersea research facility assigned as the party's first salvage target aboard the [[TSS Profondeur]]. It was abandoned after an attack by [[Undersea Extremists]] several weeks before [[Session 4 - Into the Hadal Deep]].

## Known Details

- The site has five domes.
- Three domes are broken open.
- Two damaged domes still hold oxygen.
- One power station remains active and feeds a load consistent with life support, computers, or air filtration.
- At least one airlock was blown apart by underwater explosives.
- A sealed chamber contained four living survivors and ten dead Jardin Navy personnel.

## Session 4

The party surveys the site by drone, sensors, and exterior suit work. Rather than immediately breaching a sealed oxygenated section, they build a temporary airlock and attempt a rescue.

The rescue recovers four survivors, but only one lives after transfer: the [[Mercier Lookalike Survivor]].

## Salvage Recovered

- Power generators.
- Computer equipment.
- Data cubes.
- A Seafarer skill chip.

## Open Questions

- Did the attackers know who was trapped inside?
