# Sabine Caron

![Sabine Caron portrait](/wiki/media/Sabine-Caron.png)

*Security / Diving Operations*

## Overview

Sabine Caron is the security and diving-operations lead aboard the [[TSS Profondeur]]. A former Jardin Navy marine and underwater security specialist, she carries paired sawed-off shotguns and handles combat-readiness outside the hull.

Sabine joined Transstar after killing her abusive husband and deciding that a corrupt judiciary would send her to prison for decades.

## Session 4

Sabine trains the party in undersea suit procedure, exterior movement, buddy checks, and the division between salvage hands and security. She receives Emma's transmitted warning about the hidden [[Jardinian Naval SOS]] while the party operates outside the ship.

At [[Undersea Base Elyon]], Sabine prepares the dead Jardin Navy personnel recovered from Hadal for transport to their families and the military.
