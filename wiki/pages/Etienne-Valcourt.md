# Étienne Valcourt

*Captain / Pilot*

## Overview

Étienne Valcourt is the captain and pilot of the [[TSS Profondeur]]. He spent roughly twenty years in the Jardin Navy, specializing in submersible operations and platform work, before taking a Transstar opportunity between promotion cycles.

Valcourt treats the party as useful crew if they prove they can work, learn, and avoid self-destruction. He is direct about risk, pressure, and shipboard utility, but he honors the party's rescue attempt at [[Hadal Research Site]].

## Session 4

Valcourt introduces the Profondeur and its senior crew, assigns the party into training roles, and explains the Hadal salvage mission. After the party rescues a Jardin Navy survivor from the sealed Hadal dome, he diverts to [[Undersea Base Elyon]].

In the crew mess, Valcourt points out the contradiction: Hadal was a Transstar research facility, yet every corpse and the lone survivor recovered from the sealed room wore Jardin Navy uniforms.
