# Sergey Sun

## Overview

Sergey Sun is one of [[Emma Colbert (Laura)]]'s contacts. He operates a loose smuggling network that moves restricted pharmaceutical goods, records, and occasionally people.

## Session 5

During [[Session 5 - The Oduya]], Emma identifies Sergey as the contact most suited to helping move [[Alain Mercier]] off-world or into an off-world transfer path after the party raises [[Oduya]].

## Relevance

Sergey is useful because Mercier cannot legally return to Jardin's surface after clone restoration. The party needs a route that moves him from underwater custody to orbit or Navy transfer without triggering public, legal, or genetic-control exposure.
