# Domirina

At the end of [[Session 8 - The Price of Clearing Your Name]], the party chooses to travel to a biological research center on a moon of [[Free Arden]]. The transcript renders the destination as **Dorma**; the existing system preparation in [[Jardin]] uses **Domirina**, so this page retains Domirina as the working name with Dorma as an alias pending confirmation.

The announced journey from [[North Meridian]] is roughly nine AU and eighteen to twenty hours. The crew plans to analyze the vault data during transit. It has not arrived or proven that this site manufactures the altered genetic material. Beaumont's vault key is not stated to open this facility.

:::gm
## Existing Preparation

Jardin's side-adventure material places a private biomedical installation here and proposes a physician seeking extraction with research data and evidence of her own crimes. That scenario is unplayed preparation, not a discovery made in Game 8. The opening recap also mentions a cloning lead on Sanctuary; do not silently equate the two sites.
