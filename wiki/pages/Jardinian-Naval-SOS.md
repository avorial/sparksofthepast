# Jardinian Naval SOS

## Overview

The Jardinian Naval SOS is an encrypted distress signal discovered by [[Emma Colbert (Laura)]] while training on [[TSS Profondeur]] communications. The signal is hidden from ordinary Transstar systems and uses military encryption familiar to Emma from SolSec and Jardin military work.

## Session 4

Emma realizes the signal indicates living Jardin Navy personnel at or near an attacked location. She later frames the discovery as an outside interception and transmits the information through [[Sabine Caron]].

The later rescue at [[Hadal Research Site]] confirms that Jardin Navy personnel were trapped inside a Transstar facility.

## Open Questions

- Who sent the SOS?
- Why was it hidden from normal Transstar channels?
- Did SolSec, Jardin Navy, or Transstar already know the survivors were there?
