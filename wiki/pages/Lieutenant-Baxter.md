# Lieutenant Baxter

In [[Session 8 - The Price of Clearing Your Name]], Baxter leads the armed interception near the scheduled meeting location on [[Providence]]. He is a SolSec lieutenant serving [[Director Adrian Beaumont]]'s private security team.

A warning shot provokes Saichi's defensive weapon deployment, but Baxter holsters his pistol and approaches to explain the invitation. He says the pad carriers were initially considered possible threats and that their arrival suggests a search for information. He escorts the crew by autonomous vehicle to Beaumont's office and offers the stairs if they dislike elevators.

During the later rocket attack, Baxter enters armed and moves to secure the director. His full name and later fate are not established. He is not identified as the attacker or a faction defector.
