# GL15

## Identification

In [[Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain]], [[Dr. Anaïs Bellanger]] names the scarce substance discussed since the [[Krypton]] discovery as GL15. It is associated with stable/perfect cloning and clean delivery of engineered genetic changes. It is not identified as a simple drug that automatically grants psychic powers.

## Mechanism Described in Play

Bellinger says it opens RNA pathways so precise modifications can be implemented without the severe damage associated with forcing changes by substitute methods. Without it, alterations produce fragility, decay, and further failures. Her predictive models can identify a problem but cannot safely implement every repair without causing another failure.

These are fictional in-game scientific explanations. A physical sample, formula, production method, or new stockpile is not acquired by the crew in this session.

## Investigative Importance

The shortage explains pressure to find substitutes and the facility's dependence on exploitative methods. Bellinger's locked research and testimony offer evidence, while the earlier report of an Imperial visitor to Krypton remains an unresolved separate lead.
