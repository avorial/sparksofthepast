# Roman Juma

## Overview

Roman Juma is [[Cass Pien (Kolb)]]'s crusading journalist ally. During [[Session 6 - Ghosts in the Bloodline]], the party decides Roman is too valuable and too vulnerable to send directly into [[Mercier Penthouse]], but he is ideal as a disclosure channel once the party has proof.

## Session 6 Role

Cass reveals that he has access to both an influential Solomani Party ally and Roman Juma. The party discusses whether to feed the information to Roman and have him retrieve Mercier's proof. They reject that plan because it would likely get him killed and because unproven claims would be easy to dismiss.

Instead, Roman becomes part of a dead-man switch. If Cass misses required check-ins or sends a duress code, the evidence can go out through Roman and the political ally.

## Dead-Man Switch

- Normal check-ins are discussed as 24-hour or 48-hour cycles depending on response code.
- A duress code would release the data immediately.
- Pairing a journalist channel with a political/government channel should make suppression much harder.
- Once released from outside Jardin's local lock-down authority, the data can propagate through the wider sector.
