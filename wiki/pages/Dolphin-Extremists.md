# Dolphin Extremists

## Overview

Transstar describes the Dolphin Extremists as a rogue aquatic group interfering with the [[Aquatic Salvage Project]]. The party has not yet met them directly.

## Known From Session 3

During onboarding, [[Claire Bouchard]] explains that Transstar needs military support because dolphin extremists are trying to stop the company's salvage techniques. Transstar does not care whether the group is killed, scared off, or otherwise neutralized, as long as corporate losses stop.

## Open Questions

- Are the dolphins actually extremists, or is this Transstar framing?
- What salvage work are they trying to prevent?

- Can they become allies instead of enemies?
