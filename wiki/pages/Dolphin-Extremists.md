# Dolphin Extremists

## Overview

Transstar first describes the opposition to the [[Aquatic Salvage Project]] as rogue dolphin extremists. In [[Session 4 - Into the Hadal Deep]], the briefing clarifies that the threat is broader and better tracked as [[Undersea Extremists]]: humanoids and orcas using cybernetic rigs, explosive harpoons, and deep-sea tactics.

## Known From Session 3

During onboarding, [[Claire Bouchard]] explains that Transstar needs military support because dolphin extremists are trying to stop the company's salvage techniques. Transstar does not care whether the group is killed, scared off, or otherwise neutralized, as long as corporate losses stop.

## Session 4 Clarification

The Profondeur crew describes attackers who include orcas capable of transporting cargo internally, cybernetically enhanced leaders strong enough to rip metal, and explosive micro-harpoon weapons dangerous to suits and hulls.
