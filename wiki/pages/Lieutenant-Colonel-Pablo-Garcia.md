# Lieutenant Colonel Pablo Garcia

In [[Session 8 - The Price of Clearing Your Name]], Garcia greets the Travellers after their arrival at the [[North Meridian]] base. He was not expecting an inspection, but accepts their temporary military credentials and explanation. He asks how long they will remain and supplies guards to escort them around the facility.

He is not shown discovering their real mission or opposing their departure. His personal faction allegiance is unknown. The estimate of eight to sixteen hours before the visit enters an outbound report comes from Victor's tactical assessment, not an explicit promise by Garcia.
