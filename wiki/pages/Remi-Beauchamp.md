# Rémi Beauchamp

*Sonar / Sensors / Comms*

## Overview

Rémi Beauchamp handles electrics, sonar, sensors, and communications aboard the [[TSS Profondeur]]. He is a former Jardin Navy naval-intelligence signals specialist.

His job during the party's first shift is to train anyone capable on consoles while covering whichever station remains weakest.

## Session 4

Beauchamp helps prepare the new crew for sonar and communications work during the trip to [[Hadal Research Site]]. After the rescue, he sends the emergency message to [[Undersea Base Elyon]] reporting one living unidentified Jardin Navy survivor and multiple dead from the earlier attack.
