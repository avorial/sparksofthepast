# Session 7 - Echoes Beneath the Sea

**Source:** `transcript_2026-10-06T15_17_07.340Z.txt`, supplied October 6, 2026. The opening 00:00-00:03 recap describes [[Session 6 - Ghosts in the Bloodline]]; the new action begins with the return to work. No precise in-world date is established.

## Synopsis

After their violent weekend at [[Mercier Penthouse]], the Travellers return to [[TSS Profondeur]] aboard a transport boat. [[Victor Mendes (Steinbach)]] is functional after surgery but still sore and bandaged. Captain [[Etienne Valcourt]] welcomes them with a private reckoning in the crew's mess. His whiteboard lists the rescued political clone, salvaged ship under repair in Transstar docks, and deaths of Home Guard personnel. Corporate protection now depends on demonstrating value to [[Transstar]].

Their assignment is to investigate and, if possible, neutralize the undersea attackers operating in the [[Crushed Trench]]. A reinforced eight-person [[Deep-Trench Minisub]] can descend beyond Profondeur's limits. The Travellers must operate it themselves while the regular crew mans the parent vessel. Equipped with manipulator arms, a scouting drone, and mining explosives rather than guns or torpedoes, they descend into the chasm.

[[Dan Araujo (Josh)]] initially pilots with encouragement from [[Cass Pien (Kolb)]], but scrapes a wall and raises a sediment cloud. Victor's sensors locate movement below; [[Emma Colbert (Laura)]] reports their situation to Profondeur. Dan's next maneuver slips past two dolphins and three humanoids on jet-powered skiffs, leaving the patrol investigating the collision site. As depth increases, creaks and moisture around bolts prompt a search for shelter.

The crew finds three cave entrances and chooses the middle-sized one. Dan scouts it with the drone, finding an air pocket and unusually clean walls that suggest traffic. [[Varian Svazz (Allen)]] takes over piloting and secures the minisub using a manipulator arm. [[Zoy (Damon)]] scouts ahead; the crew finds a mechanical airlock with plasticized, nonporous rock around its chamber. Opening it causes a dangerous pressure transition. Cass recognizes the danger and coaches the group through controlled suit venting and breathing. Everyone survives, but Varian takes two Endurance damage from ruptured blood vessels. The equalization displaces water and leaves the sub farther out toward the cave mouth.

Beyond the airlock lies a settlement of roughly 250-300 people, not the military base they expected. The Travellers conceal their suits, acquire or distress work clothes, and explore repair bays, transport lifts, a cafe, and computer facilities. Children wear patched adult clothing; there are few children and no teenagers. People carry very few weapons. No uplifted apes are observed. Emma's computer work reveals repurposed medical terminals and decades of continuous records. This community has existed for approximately 25-30 years.

Cass spots a younger, dark-haired counterpart of [[Judith Dumont (Carla)]]. Nearby is [[Francis Adams]], the SolSec recruiter Emma remembers dying in a car explosion. Emma approaches alone and lets Francis assume she is another rescued clone. He describes a community of discarded backups and restored people who cannot safely resume their identities on the surface. Some finance it through old bank accounts; others sell intelligence, programming, and smuggling expertise. They pay dolphin extremists and sympathetic humans to rescue more clones.

Francis also reveals that reliable cloning depends on an exhaustible resource recovered from an old Solomani site on a moon of [[Krypton]]. Supplies are dwindling. Experiments using less material produce clones with tics, seizures, and other impairments that medication can alleviate but not cure. Further research exposes another danger: one armed faction seeks to damage Transstar, while another extracts valuable memories from rescued clones. The settlement's residents understand their rescuers less fully than they believe.

The crew debates evacuation, negotiation, sabotage, and disclosure. They choose to preserve the settlement's secrecy and demolish a different cave. Emma places the explosives at the largest entrance, and Varian pilots their escape. As communications return, Profondeur reports that attackers hit a relay station and sonar detected the crew's explosion. The Travellers return without pursuing the attackers. Emma's initial deception falters, but Victor reframes her account as an executive taking responsibility for her subordinates; the explanation is accepted. The group retains Transstar cover and anticipates using [[Oduya|Volante]] during the next weekend.

## Scene Notes

### Return and Corporate Ultimatum (00:04-00:24)

- Transport to Profondeur takes about ninety minutes. Victor has advanced medical foam and bandages; surgery did not erase all discomfort.
- Valcourt waits until the transport crew is gone before confronting the party privately.
- He has been involved in their underwater transport and salvage; news of the Home Guard fight reached him through other channels. His knowledge does not establish that he betrayed them.
- He is trying to keep the party useful enough that Transstar continues protecting them instead of handing them to Jardin's military.
- Transstar's stated interest is protecting revenue from undersea laboratories and transport. The mission is corporate asset protection.
- Combative members who are recuperating or inactive do not all join the expedition. Attendance should not be inferred merely from the minisub's eight seats.
- The reinforced craft is launched from the lower-deck observation-nose area. Profondeur remains above as support and emergency recovery.

### Descent and Patrol Evasion (00:24-00:44)

- Initial assignments: Dan pilots, Victor operates sensors, Emma handles communications, Cass provides leadership.
- The GM allows spacecraft piloting at -1 using Intellect as an improvised substitute for underwater expertise.
- Cass's leadership gives Dan boons for three piloting attempts.
- The first scrape produces noise and sediment but is not a catastrophic hull failure.
- Sensors initially detect targets about 1,200 feet below the crew, then identify two dolphins and three humanoids using powered skiffs.
- Dan chooses evasion over proposed ramming. The patrol heads toward the scrape while the minisub slips past.
- Hull creaking and moisture appear as pressure increases; the group discusses monitoring compression with a string across the cabin. No completed major repair is shown.

### Cave Survey and Pressure Emergency (00:44-01:15, approximate)

- Three entrances range from a large opening, to a minibus-sized opening, to an opening likened to a sliding window. The crew explores the middle one.
- Emma establishes a repeater at the first cave's edge; communication is initially possible within about 500 yards, subject to cave penetration.
- Dan's drone travels roughly twenty feet inside before the floor rises toward an air pocket. Clean cave walls contrast with the dusty collision site.
- Varian brings the sub in and holds it at the side with a manipulator arm.
- Zoy's scouting identifies the crude mechanical airlock. The group remains suited while checking the environment.
- Suit readings show oxygen with some methane and hydrogen. The GM allows that breathing it might require an Endurance test; the crew initially keeps protective suits on.
- The airlock has wheels, gears, and pulleys, no visible electronic lock or camera, and a second door about five feet beyond the first.
- Dan and Emma's science checks identify rock treated to withstand the pressure difference.
- Opening the inner transition gives roughly 15-20 seconds before suit compromise. Cass uses his Belter experience and leadership to guide three stages of venting.
- Varian suffers two Endurance damage, bleeding from nose and eyes, while his suit remains intact. The GM says time and rest, rather than immediate treatment, will repair that particular injury; recovery is not played out here.
- These pressure effects are recorded as the game's adjudication, not real-world diving instructions.

### Infiltration and Discovery (approximately 01:15-01:50)

- The crew hides its suits in a bundle rather than leaving them conspicuously in the airlock.
- Zoy finds a crank-start oxygen refilling point, emergency cylinders, work coveralls, a repair bay, and metal stairs.
- Powered wall-side lifts move residents between levels at roughly fifteen miles per hour.
- Zoy finds additional work clothes using Streetwise. Cass, Victor, and Emma successfully distress their uniforms; spare outfits cover others whose deception is poor.
- The city has a cafe, surface news broadcasts, computer terminals, and community activities such as chess and Scrabble.
- Children are roughly one in sixty residents. Their clothing is repurposed from worn Transstar or laboratory uniforms. No teenagers are observed.
- Sidearms are rare, estimated around one person in forty. The party is more heavily armed than ordinary residents.
- A lagoon supplies drinking water. Dolphins can be encountered using walking mechanical rigs, but no uplifted apes are seen.
- Emma discovers medical computers reused as civilian terminals. Hardware is no older than about twenty years; successive databases demonstrate a longer, continuous community history.

### Familiar Faces and Francis's Account

- Cass recognizes Judith's counterpart and informs the others without first asserting that she is a clone.
- The younger Judith does not recognize Emma. Francis does recognize her, creating a different memory-history clue.
- Emma approaches alone and maintains the new-arrival cover. No confirmed physical meeting with the original Judith occurs.
- Francis works as a psychologist, helping inhabitants adjust to lost status and find purpose. His Solomani loyalty remains strong despite exile.
- Some inhabitants were awakened after an original's death. Others are surplus backups rescued even though their original never died.
- He says dolphin extremists and sympathetic humans can be paid to recover new residents, and describes criminal work as a source of survival income.
- New arrivals use temporary accommodation before permanent housing is assigned. A recent arrival group came about a month earlier.
- Francis believes Emma's original was killed after the assassination frame-up; she supports that misunderstanding and says she was framed.
- He warns that contacting family or retaining known identities has drawn [[Social Solidarity]] attempts to locate the community.
- The city's information relay changes location and retransmits about every six hours. Supplies also arrive through underground visitors.
- Francis recommends mushroom burgers and computer facilities that teach hacking and data analysis.

### Library Findings and Wider Conspiracy

- Research confirms Transstar does **not** know about the clone population. It assumes stolen clones could not survive transport, oxygen shortages, and extreme depth.
- The armed opposition has two factions: one attacks Transstar; another gathers valuable information from clone memories. The civilian settlement is a third, separate group.
- Residents think rescue is a transaction they fund; some rescuers have an additional information-extraction agenda.
- A dolphin underworld contact encountered in Game 1 is supplying the settlement. The transcript does not securely identify that contact by personal name.
- Other cloning worlds do not have a confirmed direct communication network with this city. Comparable diversion of valuable clones is plausible, but specific sister cities are not confirmed.
- Residents fear genetic identification if they leave. Some people do escape; criminal visitors sometimes kidnap residents. Kidnapping is not a trustworthy evacuation system.
- Records show clones live at most about twenty-five years after creation, regardless of their apparent age.
- Their brains enter a low-activity, near-hibernation sleep state rather than ordinary active dreaming. This was designed to reduce wear.
- Ordinary genetic matching cannot readily distinguish original and clone. Aging cells and increasing their metabolism reveals subtle discrepancies that could otherwise look like laboratory error.
- Residents are not systematically testing their own genetic purity. Emma downloads a genetic listing to her brain computer for later analysis.

### Decision, Demolition, and Return (02:30-02:56, approximate)

- The group rejects revealing the city to Transstar or flooding its inhabited airlock.
- Evacuation is discussed but not performed. Oduya has roughly twenty tons of cargo space; a theoretical forty low berths is a capacity calculation, not installed equipment.
- Emma proposes changing or deleting navigation logs to protect the city's coordinates and requests leadership help. No completed edit or successful check is explicitly recorded.
- The crew votes to bomb a separate cave. Emma uses Explosives 0 and chooses the largest entrance after Carla's table-level suggestion; that suggestion does not establish Judith's in-world participation.
- The roll is unfavorable, but the GM confirms an explosion and calls for a quick getaway. No confirmed casualty count or inspected demolition result follows.
- Varian successfully pilots out. Profondeur says a relay attack caused their communication loss and reports hearing the explosion on sonar.
- Returning attackers use devices that atomize surrounding H2O, creating brief gaps through which they descend faster. The crew does not chase them or deploy a completed drone pursuit.
- Emma's first cover-story roll is poor. Victor's explanation that she is claiming responsibility for her team's work is strongly believed.
- The expedition ends aboard Profondeur. A weekend departure in Oduya is anticipated, not yet played.
