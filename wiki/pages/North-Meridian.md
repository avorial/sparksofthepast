# North Meridian

## Played Events

In [[Session 8 - The Price of Clearing Your Name]], the party reaches North Meridian after leaving [[Providence]]. Victor's course reduces the stated flight time from about seven hours to two. Around two-thirds of the world is intensely tectonically active; a more stable region still suffers earthquakes. The atmosphere is described as methane and helium without breathable oxygen. Suits are necessary outside.

There is a mining operation/trading post and a separate guarded military base. The party lands near the base, checks suits, and presents temporary military authorization under a surprise-inspection cover. Guards use radio frequency 4216. [[Lieutenant Colonel Pablo Garcia]] accepts their presence and gives them an escort.

## Intelligence Site

The [[North Meridian Intelligence Vault]] holds sensitive security and undercover-personnel records, political dossiers, and intelligence on the dolphin criminal network. [[Director Adrian Beaumont]] provides the party's key. The crew leaves with copies of the required personnel data and additional offered dossiers without fighting the garrison.

The proposed industrial side adventure in [[Jardin]]'s GM material is preparation, not evidence that its mining dispute occurred in this game.
