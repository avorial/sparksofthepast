# Crushed Trench

The aquatic map identifies this as a deep tectonic rift with extreme pressure. In [[Session 7 - Echoes Beneath the Sea]], [[TSS Profondeur]] deploys a [[Deep-Trench Minisub]] because the parent vessel cannot safely reach suspected bases at the bottom.

## Explored Approaches

The party detects two dolphins and three humanoids on powered skiffs. Noise and sediment from a wall scrape attract the patrol, which the party evades. Three caves offer large, medium, and very small entrances. The medium entrance leads to an air pocket, mechanical airlock, and [[Hidden Clone Community]]. The largest entrance is later bombed as a substitute target; no full interior survey or confirmed casualty report follows.

## Armed Opposition

Research distinguishes two factions within the [[Undersea Extremists]]: anti-Transstar attackers and a group exploiting valuable clone memories. The city is a separate civilian population. Returning attackers use devices described as atomizing H2O to create short-lived gaps for rapid descent. The crew does not pursue them.

Communications initially work through Emma's repeater, then fail during an attack on a relay station reported by Profondeur. The source does not name that station, so its identity as [[Relay Station 932]] remains unconfirmed.
