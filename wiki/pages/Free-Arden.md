# Free Arden

Free Arden is a gas giant in the Jardin system. In [[Session 8 - The Price of Clearing Your Name]], the next destination is [[Domirina]], called Dorma in the recording, a moon associated with biological research. The quoted distance from North Meridian is about nine AU, with an eighteen-to-twenty-hour trip. The crew has not reached the moon by the close of the game.

:::gm
## System Preparation

[[Jardin]]'s GM-only side adventures propose commercial fuel operations at Free Arden and substantial corporate or clandestine activity on its moons. Those hooks remain proposed until encountered in play.
:::
