# Free Arden

## Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain

[[Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain]]

A distressed merchant, [[Snowball]], hides inside the gas giant for twenty-two hours. The crew relays its call under a SolSec disguise; [[HMS Hank Aaron]] destroys the pirate pursuer. The party then covertly lands on the second moon, infiltrates the research facility, and departs with Bellinger. No refueling or docking transaction is played.

Free Arden is a gas giant in the Jardin system. In [[Session 8 - The Price of Clearing Your Name]], the next destination is [[Domirina]], called Dorma in the recording, a moon associated with biological research. The quoted distance from North Meridian is about nine AU, with an eighteen-to-twenty-hour trip. The crew has not reached the moon by the close of the game.
