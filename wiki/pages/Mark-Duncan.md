# Mark Duncan

## Overview

Mark Duncan is one of [[Emma Colbert (Laura)]]'s compartmentalized contacts, connected to travel clearance, restricted travel grants, or spaceport movement records. In [[Session 6 - Ghosts in the Bloodline]], Emma uses him to identify the passenger tied to [[Alain Mercier]]'s Providence ticket.

## Session 6 Role

Emma sends Mark the flight and seat data from [[Mercier's Locked Pad]] under a cover identity rather than as herself. He asks what she needs it for and warns that the request could get him in trouble. Emma answers vaguely that the information could blow a case open.

About ten minutes later, Mark returns a record and photo. The passenger is [[Camille Dufour]], an encrypted-identity SolSec agent.

## Notes

- Mark does not necessarily know Emma as Emma.
- He is useful for travel, transport, and clearance questions.
- He is now owed a significant favor.
- His access is valuable but dangerous to overuse.
