# Francis Adams

## Identity

[[Emma Colbert (Laura)]] remembers Francis as the man who recruited her into [[SolSec]] and later died in a car explosion. In [[Session 7 - Echoes Beneath the Sea]], she meets a counterpart in the [[Hidden Clone Community]]. He recognizes Emma and assumes she is a rescued clone whose original was killed after the Mercier frame-up. Emma sustains this cover. His precise backup date is not established.

## Work and Outlook

Francis is a psychologist helping residents adapt to lost status, exile, and the meaning of a second life. He remains loyal to Solomani national ideals, describing the inhabitants as pioneers exploring echoes of their existence. He believes even an impaired second life can have purpose.

## Information Given to Emma

- Residents include both posthumous restorations and surplus backups whose originals never died.
- Dolphin extremists and sympathetic humans recover clones in exchange for payment.
- Old finances and remotely useful criminal skills support the city.
- New arrivals receive temporary housing; one group arrived about a month before the meeting.
- Reliable cloning uses a finite resource from a site on a moon of [[Krypton]]. Reduced-resource experiments cause tics, seizures, and other impairments; medication limits symptoms without curing them.
- Around eight or nine major Solomani worlds have cloning facilities. This is an approximate account, not a complete facility directory.
- Surface broadcasts are relayed about every six hours from changing locations. Underground visitors bring supplies.
- Contacting family under old identities has drawn [[Social Solidarity]] searches.

Francis is a potential source and ally, but he does not knowingly learn that Emma is the original visiting on a Transstar mission. His account does not prove he knows the rescuers' memory-extraction agenda.
