# Session 3 - Transstar Day

**Transcript Source:** `transcript_2026-07-28T13_25_40.538Z.json`

**Caption File:** `wiki/transcripts/Session-3-Transstar-Day.srt`

## Synopsis

With the [[Identity Veils]] almost expired, the group chooses a path that is neither full surrender nor full Syndicate employment. They send the recovered Mercier evidence to [[Inspector Marc Valois]] through [[Lucin]], then retreat into the [[Transstar Sector Exchange]] before their disguises fail.

Lucin receives the [[Mobile Data Tube]] evidence, related files, and physical proof at [[La Porte Fiel]]. He agrees to have the package placed inside Valois's home later that night, using the favor owed from the Session 2 delivery as payment. During the handoff, [[Mercier's Locked Pad]] activates a tracking signal. Lucin seals it in a Faraday bag, and the group escapes before police reach the club.

At the Exchange, [[Reena Kessler]] grants three-day work-seeker stamps to the first arrivals. The party learns that Transstar employment creates a corporate legal shield inside the Exchange and aboard Transstar ships. Once the Lucin team escapes, everyone brings their gear inside before the veils fail.

The group takes Transstar hiring tests. Emma succeeds spectacularly on the executive track and becomes a division-level executive. Others become loaders or corporate security after deciding how far to push their luck. [[Claire Bouchard]] handles onboarding, gives them employee badges with their real faces, and assigns the group to the [[Aquatic Salvage Project]].

The party is now temporarily safe as Transstar employees, housed together in Emma's executive suite. The price is corporate supervision, an underwater salvage job, and a thirty-day clock before they must either clear their names, leave, or accept a longer Transstar term.

## What Happened

The session opened with a planning argument in Emma's warehouse. The group wanted to avoid becoming criminals in truth while still surviving the end of the [[Identity Veils]]. Options included contacting [[Marcel Delphine]], pressing [[Dr. Luc Moreau]], reaching [[Inspector Marc Valois]], finding new veils, or moving to the [[Transstar Sector Exchange]].

The party settled on a compromise: get the evidence to Valois without exposing themselves. Ciernan led the Lucin team back to Sainte-Liberte. They found Lucin inside [[La Porte Fiel]], a drug den where he was calmly searching customers' pockets while they were too intoxicated to react. Lucin accepted the job, scrubbed the package for DNA, and arranged a home delivery to Valois.

As the evidence was sealed, [[Mercier's Locked Pad]] activated a "Find My Data Pad" signal. Lucin put the pad in a Faraday bag and told the group to leave. Ciernan guided the escape, and the team got away before police arrived.

At the same time, Emma, Victor, Dan, and Varian entered the Exchange. [[Reena Kessler]] explained the three-day work-seeker stamp and the path from visitor to Transstar employee. Once inside, the group saw [[Transstar Peacekeepers]] and realized corporate law would replace Jardin law inside the Exchange.

The first group called the others and told them to come immediately with all gear. Saichi entered with a restrainer tag that powered down her guns. Zoy entered through a mix of confusion and confidence, without receiving a normal stamp.

The party then gambled on employment tests. The tests measured skill and risk judgement: pushing to a higher tier after a success could lead to promotion, but failing after pushing too far could drop the applicant to loader status. Emma became a division-level executive. Several others became loaders or corporate security. [[Claire Bouchard]] then folded everyone into Transstar orientation.

The onboarding terms give the party thirty days of open employment. During that window, they may leave or be fired. After that, Transstar expects a longer two-, four-, or six-year commitment. The first assignment is [[Aquatic Salvage Project]], with corporate housing in Emma's suite and work beginning at 4 a.m.
