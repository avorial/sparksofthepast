# Commandant Rémi Leduc

![Commandant Rémi Leduc portrait](/wiki/media/Remi-Leduc.jpg)

*Security Chief*

Staff records examined in [[Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain]] identify the commander of the [[Free Arden Research Facility]] as a former SolSec security specialist who spent roughly ten years protecting black sites before advancing to the current role. The original roster confirms Commandant Rémi Leduc; Remiluk/Remy Luke/Remy Luc are transcript variants preserved as aliases.

Email chains visible through Bellinger's account show researchers avoiding or working around him. This does not establish that he knows the party is present. He is not directly encountered, killed, or identified as the gun-wielding defender at the exit.

:::gm
## Original GM Profile

**Role:** Security Chief | **Age:** 47 | **Species:** Human.

Courteous, controlled, and ideologically committed to the Social Solidarity splinter faction. The original preparation gives him oversight of shipments of modified material and knowledge of psychic replacements. He wants the crew's evidence, its sponsor's identity, and an assessment of whether Zoy is genuinely psychic. No such direct interrogation occurred in Game 9.

**Suggested Traveller skills:** Gun Combat (Energy) 2, Investigate 2, Recon 2, Leadership 2, Tactics (Military) 2, Deception 2.
