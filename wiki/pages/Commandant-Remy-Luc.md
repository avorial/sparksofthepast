# Commandant Remy Luc

Staff records examined in [[Session 9 - The Doctor's Bargain]] identify the commander of the [[Free Arden Research Facility]] as a former SolSec security specialist who spent roughly ten years protecting black sites before advancing to the current role. The recording renders his name Remiluk/Remy Luke; Remy Luc is a working spelling.

Email chains visible through Bellinger's account show researchers avoiding or working around him. This does not establish that he knows the party is present. He is not directly encountered, killed, or identified as the gun-wielding defender at the exit.
